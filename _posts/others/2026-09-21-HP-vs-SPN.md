Fun fact: Harry Potter was born one year after Dean Winchester, and three years before Sam Winchester. (Dean 1979, Harry 1980, Sam 1983)

The date that Mary Winchester died, was two years and two days after the day James and Lily Potter died. (Potters 1981.10.31, Mary 1983.11.2)

(I know we hate JKR and stop talking about HP, but bear with me, I just found out this, it's so fascinating and hilarious to see they really are about the same age, and as soon as you realize there is a real timeline that can be lined up, I have to do a cross-comparison, just to see what they are doing at the same time. It's incredible to see how life has been treating all of them, and how similar and different that is. And trust me, this is not about Harry Potter at all. Read to the end.)

(A quick note: The Winchester childhood part mostly comes from "John Winchester's Journal," plus what little bit we get from the show's hints and flashbacks. I consider the book mostly canon, though there is a small discrepancy with the show because the journal was early; I used the journal version instead of the show's version, you'll see it when it comes.)

<br>

When Harry received his school letter and then went to school even though his uncle and aunt didn't allow him to, Dean was supposed to go to sixth grade but his father pulled him out of school because they had to move again. (1991.9.1)

The Christmas that Harry spent at school with his two new best friends, doing research on Medieval mythology, and he received Christmas gifts from his friends and friends' family, Dean and Sam spent alone in a motel, waiting for their father that didn't come back, and Dean pretended their dad had come back and gave his brother stolen gifts, and Sam gave him the amulet. (1991.12.25)

The time Harry almost died saving the Philosopher's Stone, Dean and Sam almost died from a skinwalker. (1992.5)

Harry went to his uncle and aunt's home for summer vacation, and he hid his magic wand under the bed; that same summer, Sam woke up in the middle of the night, scared of the thing in the closet, and his father gave him a .45 to put under his pillow, and told him that he'd know what to do next time he was scared of the thing in the closet. (1992.6.21)

Harry started having feelings for Ginny; at the same time, Dean started going to movies with girlfriends, and John commented in his journal that Dean was popular with girls. And Dean also killed a Baba Yaga that year. (1993.1.24)

Harry found the Chamber of Secrets and figured out the mythology of the Basilisk and killed it; at the same time, Sam and Dean were pulled out of school by John because he needed to take them to train in bowhunting. (1993.5.2)

Harry sneaked to Hogsmeade for a fun Christmas with friends with a magic invisibility cloak inherited from his father; the same year, on Christmas, Dean and Sam stole a theosophy book from a store to give to their father as the Christmas gift. (1993.12.25)

Harry started learning about his father's childhood friends' secrets and was about to meet all his father's childhood friends; around the same time, Dean helped his father take out a haunted spirit. (1994.1.24)

Harry learned he had a godfather and possibly a home to go to; at the same time, John Winchester was grieving it would have been his sixteenth marriage anniversary had Mary been there, and they had been on the road all these years. (1994.5.17)

Harry got selected in an international monster-fighting competition and had to face dragons, mermaids, and a sphinx; around the same time, Dean was asked by his father to lead a werewolf hunt. (1995.1.24)

Harry witnessed Cedric get killed and the man who killed his parents resurrected using his blood; around the same time, Dean was sent to a boys' home for two months for stealing bread, and their father still had no idea what killed their mom, with only some guesses. (1995 summer)

Harry started getting grumpy and having nightmares and visions from the soul connection with the man who killed his parents and threw tantrums at all his friends; at the same time, Dean was sent to his first solo hunt to handle ghosts of two nuns, and it made him sick. (1996.1.24)

The summer Harry lost his godfather due to him being him and reckless and not listening to orders; Dean had his best memory of having Independence Day fireworks with Sam, and they burned down a field. (1996.7.4)

Harry used his mom's old textbook to get a good grade in class; Dean received his 18th birthday gift, which was his dad's car, the Impala. (1997.1.24)

Harry continued to have visions from the soul connection with the man who killed his parents; Sam had been having strange dreams again from the blood given to him by the thing that killed their mom. (1997.5.2)

Harry left school; Dean graduated high school and got his diploma. (1997.6.16)

The day of the Battle of Hogwarts; was the day of Sam Winchester's fifteenth birthday, and John thought about sending him to solo hunt just like he did to Dean when he was fifteen. (1998.5.2)

Harry joined the Auror Office after the war; Dean had been doing solo hunts for quite some time now. (1998)

Harry married Ginny and had three kids throughout the years; Dean had a brief relationship with Cassie and then they broke up, spent a weekend at Lisa's and then he left. (early 2000s)

Harry became Head of the Auror Office; Dean made the demon deal to save Sam and had one last year to live. (2007)

Harry's youngest kid started school, and he and the woman he loves sent all three of their kids to school together, and his scar hasn't hurt for 19 years, and all was good and happy for him; the angel who saved him from Hell was killed before Dean's eyes and Dean had to burn his body and spread his ashes -- at this point in time, Cas has been gone for four months and wouldn't be back until after another two months, and Dean consistently wanted to kill himself. (2017.9.1)
