Another special episode like Season 1's *A Thousand Cat's Dream*, a bit surprised to see Colin Morgan here. Still familiar face from *Merlin*, though more like *Benjamin* this time, and almost feel like a stereotype casting this time. Another suicidal cynical journalist, and the suicidal reason is because of the depressing hopeless environmental problem... He does have a depressing suicidal look just the way he is. Wouldn't be surprised if it's an offering instead of casting. He's good at this type of roles.

And Death is good as ever. The story is just very... predictable. Death loves life, and somehow her enthusiasm and love of life taught some human some lesson and make them want to value life more.

The Mad Hattie storyline is a little of surprise. The death of the human Death scene is beautiful, the heartbeat sound in the sound effect, the last breath, the somber music, and then the falling back and splash. It's a beautiful death scene. The two coins on the eyes made it slightly unsettling, but a nice tribute to the Greek tradition (I assume).

Wouldn't say it's tooooo outstanding, but not too bad either. I'd give it a mediocre rating.

<br>

So, there are all the episodes of the second season, and I stand by my initial overall thoughts after the second round of rewatch (aka exactly my feelings when I finished the first time): I love the show, but it's not a super good one. The story is chaotic in the most typical Gaiman way, the character curve is depressing, the MCD warning is heartbreaking, but the music, the song of Orpheus, is able to compensate every single aspect of the defects and stay in my mind in a special place for forever. Let alone how beautiful Lord Morpheus always look like.

Good or bad, I love Lord Morpheus. More so after knowing there won't be any more of him on the screen. Meaning the only place I'd see him is from the rest of the comics (and in my dream). Without Season 3 to come, I'll just *have to* read all the Sandman comics now.

And next to watch: *Dead Boy Detective*, heard it from Ruth Connell (SPN Rowena) first, but then found out it's also from the Sandman universe. No idea what's it about, and pretty sure it won't have anything/anyone from the Sandman. But who knows, maybe it will surprise me, maybe there're easter eggs to find.

Farewell, Lord Morpheus. See you in my dreams.
