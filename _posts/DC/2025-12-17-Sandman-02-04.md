The new quest begins, Delirium drags Morpheus to look for Destruction. The organization of this season is quite clear, with one main plot point following another (just like season 1: get out of cage, after that find the three items, after that find the three escaping dream arcanas, after that handle the vortex), and for this season, it's to free Nada (side quest: handle Hell's key), after that go find Destruction with Delirium (premise: find the three friends of Destruction's), after that handle the situation with Orpheus and the Kindly One. (Why it's always "three"? Three items, three arcanas, three friends.)

Meet Destruction for the first time. Is it just me or he really does look like a young Hemsworth brother; got so distracted so many times just thinking how much alike he is to one of them.

Love Destruction's talking dog. TV rule: get a lovable pet in the show, it's the easiest way for people to love the show.
