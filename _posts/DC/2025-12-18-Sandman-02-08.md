A person full of despair would end up in Despair's realm. Morpheus is full of despair so he shows up and greeted by Despair. Clever use of personification again.

We don't get to see Despair much, but her words are very wise:

> Despair: If I had a sister called Despair, I don't think I'd spend much time with her either. But... people need a place to go where they can be miserable. And grieve. And hit bottom. Before they... swim up for air, and start breathing again. That's what happens next.

Love the greeny underwater world in Despair's realm.

> Morpheus: I have not been the brother I should have been.
>
> Despair: Well... you're here now.
>
> Morpheus: Not for much longer.
>
> Despair: You don't know that.
>
> Morpheus: Perhaps there will be a better Dream to take my place.
>
> Despair: You're talking rubbish.

"Perhaps there will be a better Dream to take my place"

Despair, desperate, helpless, vulnerable, that's the Morpheus I'm familiar with, I feel for, I fall for.

<br>

Johanna Constantine again, yet another different one, all with the same name and same face but different era, the setting is just... too forceful. Jenna Coleman is good, sure, but just changing her clothes and hair and call her a different person, isn't it just a little bit... treating audience like we're make-believe kids.

Plot-wise they just want a character to help Morpheus out and has the characterization of John Constantine, and they can't do the Hob Godling thing again to make him/her immortal, so now it becomes the infinite generational thing but still always same face same accent same name, it's just... no way to rationalize it. They should figure out a better setting for her.

But also can see another reason of not doing the Hob Godling immortal thing: that would mean to make a long-term relationship again for Morpheus, and not good for creating his lonesome friend-less profile. Morpheus isn't good at having any kind of relationship or friendship, nor is any Constantine.

But they never explain reincarnation or anything similar, or maybe it's a magic/spell thing that Constantine knows. Or maybe they are all the same person but without memory every hundred years.

Love the small touch of the one that saved Orpheus in 18th century France ends up having her grave next to Orpheus's temple. Can see something between she and Orpheus, the teeny tiny spark is warm but not strong enough be anything deeper, but still beautiful.

<br>

Notice the Mad Hattie now when Daniel went missing. Sorta forget her setting, but remembered a dropping line from Death early on, talking about there're about sixty human left on Earth who's seen Mammoth, and Mad Hattie is one of them. Need a comic book reminder what's the deal with her. (The special episode also has her, doesn't it?)

<br>

"You want to be punished." "You're not here to find Daniel, you're on a suicide mission." There you are, Constantine, you said it. It takes a suicidal one to see through another. My sweet suicidal babies, my ultimate favorite trait.

Who doesn't like a little self-punishing? Especially the dying part.

<br>

Unsurprisingly Morpheus goes back to see Hob to say goodbye. Almost like a routine for him now, just like in S02E01. No wonder not even Hob is surprised.

> Morpheus: Hello, Hob.
>
> Hob: Hello, you. (Love how he doesn't even address him, just calling Morpheus "you".)
>
> Morpheus: I was sorry to hear of your loss.
>
> Hob: It's okay. People die. Well, most people. I've seen so many people die, my wives, my children. It never gets any easier, the people you love not being here anymore. (How would he ever know even Endless would die, he got one immortal Endless as a friend and he'd have to lose him too.)
>
> Morpheus: I must return to my realm. I'm not sure when... if ever, I will come back to this one. So I wanted to say goodbye.
>
> Hob: You do this, you know. You show up, you tell me I'm never going to see you again, and then you always come back.
>
> Morpheus: Not this time.
>
> Hob: I don't believe you. But if that's the case, shouldn't we have one last drink before you go?

Didn't realize at the time, but "having a drink" is the codeword for "let's talk with real words and let me understand if there's any way I can help save you."

> Hob: So what's this all about? Something wrong? You in trouble?
>
> Morpheus: I've made mistakes. The same mistakes. Again and again, somehow. In spite of myself.
>
> Hob: You just described what's it like to be human. My therapist calls it checking your work. Yes, it's taken me 600 years, but I am finally in therapy. And Jesse -- Jesse is my therapist -- he says it's like putting a record on. And the needle looks like it's staying in the same place, but it's actually getting closer to the center with every turn.
>
> Morpheus: [sigh] When first we met... you were a wager. Between me and my sister.
>
> Hob: What was the wager?
>
> Morpheus: That no sensible creature would crave an eternity of human life.
>
> Hob: Sorry I lost you that bet.
>
> Morpheus: No. *I'm* sorry. For your loss. For all your losses. It was not right. (He's apologizing again. He tries to fix everything before going.)
>
> Hob: Oh stop. You didn't force this on me. You offered. I accepted. No regret.
>
> Morpheus: I wish I could say the same.
>
> Hob: Look... I've been around a bit. Not as long as you, obviously. But I have never seen anyone lead a perfect life. The best any of us can do is... enjoy what little time we have. And try not to ruin it for anyone else. And whatever mistakes you've made, or you think you've made... maybe it's not too late to fix them.

"whatever mistakes you've made, or you think you've made", Hob knows him so well. How many mistakes are just "what he thinks he's made"?

~~Though, I'd never expect the very next thing he goes to do, to "fix", is to make the second Corinthian. I didn't know he consider it his mistake to punish the Corinthian. (The sweetness of the shipping right there.)~~

(Never mind, mistakes it for Corinthian, it's actually Alex. And now I can totally see where that shipping between Alex and Morpheus comes from. Alex adores him so much as a child, and fears him so much when he goes to forgive him and free him. It's so touching to see Morpheus fixing the few things that he considers mistakes.)

<br>

The very very interesting interaction between Puck and Loki. Puck is so definitely in love with Loki. And he just "accidentally" confessed to the disguised Loki.

> Loki!Morpheus: Give me the boy.
>
> Puck: On one condition. Release Loki from his obligation. The deal you made with him was not a fair one, as it requires him to be other than he is. He is not, nor will he ever be responsible, principled, or honorable in any way.
>
> Loki!Morpheus: And why risk my displeasure to save him?
>
> Puck: Because he's clever. And he makes me laugh. Well, I don't know about you, but I get a bit lonely being the smartest person in every room, so it's nice having someone who forces you to raise your game.
>
> Loki!Morpheus: (smile a little) So, if I release Loki from our agreement... you will give me the boy?
>
> Puck: I will.
>
> Loki!Morpheus: Very well, Loki is free from his obligation from this day forward.
>
> Puck: Thank you, Lord Shaper. (to Daniel) All right, Danny. Your friend Dream here is going to take you back to your mum. I really hope we get to see each other again soon.
>
> Loki: You will. I can't believe you fell for that. Or that you were so nice about me. You're not in love with me, are you?
>
> Puck: Not at the moment, no.
>
> Loki: Well, let's go home and see if I can change your mind about that.

"You're not in love with you, are you?" and "Not at the moment, no", oh sweet jeez, the romantic spark and tension. Yes he is, of course Puck has already fell, isn't "not at the moment" enough of a confession? He's so deep into Loki already but he's also still Robin Goodfellow the faerie, as much as he's mischievous, he's not evil. And Puck is so torn apart now by Loki's charm and bordering evil. It's so beautiful (and painful for him).

It's so easy to imagine a husband-husband family raising the child.

<br>

Now finally is the time for the second Corinthian and the interesting team-up with Constantine. Remember him too well, can't wait to see him again.
