## Main

Castiel flaps his wings once, and the house in which the hunters were gathering disappears from his view. He does not know where he's going, his mind is still replaying the past few weeks, maybe actually it was just a few days, or was it a few hours? He feels his sense of time is muddled ever since he woke up to the thundering of the Word, but that was weeks/days/hours ago.

Now he's thinking about words. Words swim in his mind like color on an oil painting. He remembers someone yelling at him "you broke everything you touch", and he winces. His wings flinch and his flight drop some height. The mountain wind brushes the primary feathers, and he looks down. It looks like Alps to him from this angle but he can't be sure. He flaps his wings again. He isn't even really sure what he was being yelled at for, did he break some stone, or did he break some human? Or was it himself? He doesn't feel like broken, or maybe it's been broken for so long that he never realized it. The white hospital gown under the trench coat flows with the wind. He doesn't know where he's going, or if there is anywhere he can go, just anywhere quiet, anywhere far away, anywhere he doesn't have to hear those echoes of sounds and words in his head. So he flies.

"It's all your fault." The words catch up to him quicker than his wings are able to carry him away. The words are in Enochian this time, and someone with grand wings loom over him, glaring at him with raging red eyes. Instantly chills flow through his spines. He wants to curl up in the corner, hide behind the table. He wants to hold his ears and close his eyes. He wants to disappear, he wants to stay away. *Stop.* He thinks. *Stop this.* He pleads, not noticing he plunges into a thick stormy cloud. The shadow of the gloomy cloud surrounds him, pulling him back. Was this something happened recently? Or was it so long ago? He can't tell. This is the eon after his existence, or this might as well be the seconds after his existence. He's young, he's old, and he's scared.

"Stop, stop this!" The high-pitched sound echoes in his head. The sound is so real and familiar, Castiel blinks hard. In his mind's eyes he sees the vast wings spreading, adding to the two or three that were already entangled with each other. This one is closer to him, his back to him. It's his favorite brother, he thinks, the youngest among the oldest brothers -- Gabriel. But it can't be right. He doesn't remember this ever happened. He doesn't remember being shielded, or anyone jumping in to stop them. And it can't be real, because Gabriel was dead, wasn't he? Two years at the verge of Apocalypse, trying to help them.

"Stop --" The sound comes again, shaking away the phantom of the past. The dark shadow of the cloud is too thick to be natural, he finally starts to realize. Castiel frowns, making an effort to pull his power together and stare through them. The voice echoes in his head, clearer now, more painful by every minute. He can even hear the bleeding and pain just from the voice.

Castiel draws his wings in, lowering the height. The cloud thickens as he gets closer to the center of the darkness, so does the smell of Sulfur. This is no cloud at all, he realizes, probably a little too late. It's a den of demons, away from Hell.

The demons start laughing in shrieking sound, claws and teeth extending from the dark smoke. The stormy swirl spins faster in the center of the cloud, dragging him in.

Castiel isn't sure if he can fight this, but when he searches his sleeves, silvery blade slides into his palms. And with the familiar feeling of those handles, the rest feel automatic. He doesn't need to think when he swings, so he simply lets his body do what it remembers. He swings left and the demons screams in pain, he swings right and the dark cloud retreats from him in bawling.

The cloud of demons thins gradually, and he descends. In the middle of their protection, it's a building that almost looks plain, like any plain office building that humans managed to construct for the past centuries. Except with no windows or doors. And he can't see through the walls.

The sound echoing in his head comes directly from within. Castiel drops on top of the building and draws his wings in.

A second voice comes, this time to his ear, not directly to his mind. The voice is sharp and laughing. "Do you know what's the best thing about his place, Gabriel?" A muffled sound answers but there is no words. Then, "See, Odin created this cell for Loki. So don't even bother trying reaching for help in your little angel radio. No one is crazy enough to come this far north to hear it, and no one will even think about looking for you since you were already dead!" The laughter that followed after sounds even more dangerous and preposterous than the words. The muffled sound comes more frantic after that.

Castiel puts his palms on the ceiling of the building, pouring his power into the brick. The walls and ground start shaking. But the building must have been strengthened by magic, as none of the brick breaks under the surge of power.

But that is enough for anyone inside to hear him.

The voice inside the building stops laughing. "Looks like we have company now," the voice says, with a hint of amusement. "And if my guards didn't stop it, I'd bet it's a little lost bird. What do you say we bring it in for some fun?"

Before Castiel can stand up, a strong dark force bursts out from under his feet, flushes him off balance, and drags him down before he can regain his balance. He falls in the darkness for a distance a thousand times longer than how tall the building looks like from outside. And when he crashes to the floor, all he can hear is the sound of his broken bones in his human body. He moans in the sharp pain from his human vessel, but it's nothing he can't heal in just a few minutes.

"Well, well, a guest," the owner of the voice steps around him, in white shoes and white suits.
