They bicker a lot. Dean starts noticing. Especially after Cas came back from Empty and they talked things out. It felt like taking forever, but eventually, *eventually*, some things got sorted out, some feelings got confessed. All is good and they get together. So yeah, he'd say it should be happily ever after since then, but not really, they bicker so much that he'd sometimes worry if that's the end of the world again. Like today.

It's a silly cause, really. They were arguing about the color for Jack's bedroom wall. Then it gets escalated. Then some not-so-friendly comments get thrown around. Then the comments turn into insults. Then the argument turns into a fight. The real kind. The worst kind that they started digging old wounds. Dean's already grimacing when he sensed it's going that way, but his damn pride can't allow him to be the only one being blamed for some old shit, so he's throwing some shit back too. But also, his brain is still online enough that he can't allow his mouth to spit out *real* hurtful things that he knows Cas would really get hurt enough to be silenced instantly -- he'd never *really* hurt Cas. Even though they're pretty pissed at each other at the moment.

After another slightly nasty and angry comment from Cas about Dean's unfriendly nicknames and curses, Dean returns the favor by pointing a finger at him: "Well, you're the one to talk. You called me 'hairless ape'!"

Cas blinks. "That's ten years ago."

Dean raises his eyebrows. "Okay -- you called me 'hairless ape' ten years ago!"

Cas blinks again, then frowns. "If we're counting the old times, then you called me 'junkless' twelve years ago, and 'Ken doll', before I knew the meaning of it. Isn't that worse?"

Now it's Dean's turn to blink. His breath falls short for a second. "Well, alright, I stay corrected. You're definitely not junkless." Dean's eyes slide downward uncontrollably. He gives a cheesy whistle for good measure. "Your junk is..." he chuckles, a little bashful, but not embarrassed to the slightest, "-- pretty impressive. I can testify that."

The tension vaporizes between them. The tense expression on Cas's face turns into a little tilted tip of his lip. "I stay corrected, too," Cas answers, parroting his words. "You're not, in fact, 'hairless'." On purpose or not, his eyes also briefly glance downward a few inches, to the place where it usually should be counted as socially unacceptable and rude, but that's his boyfriend he's eyeing, so, sue him. "I can testify that too." Dean feels his face turning hot extremely rapidly, but Cas is still talking. "Though it's definitely less than your ancestors."

Dean lets out a loud groan. "Dude, seriously? Is this dirty talk? Jesus Christ, we really absolutely need to take some proper dirty talk classes."

"I'm not aware such classes exist," Cas replies with a straight face.

Dean cracks so hard that all arguments are forgotten. The only thing he sees is Cas smiling with him. So all is good again.
