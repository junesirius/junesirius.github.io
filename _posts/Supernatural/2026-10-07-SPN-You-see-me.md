**Summary:**

You said, you know how I see myself. You said you know how our enemies see me.

And then you said, that's not how you see me. Because --

You see me.

It's not the first time someone told me they love me.

But it's the first time someone told me they see me.

I'm thinking about your words that night, again. And I play them in my head, over and over again. I'm afraid once I stop, I'll forget them, I'll forget you -- I'll forget the way your voice trembles, the way your eyes sparkle but still stare at me without blinking, the way you smile, the way you cry. That's the only thing I have now. I don't even have a trench coat to take with me or to burn this time. I have nothing.

I'm thinking about what you said, one word at a time. I wrote them down long ago. I don't want to get them wrong, not any single word you said. But what does that matter? I look at them, I write them again and rewrite them again, but so many things still don't make sense, not in my head, anyway. Not the way I see myself, just like you said, man, I need you here telling me those things when I can't see them myself. I -- I need you here.

I don't need people to love me. Not really.

I need people to see me.

But I'm so afraid for people to see me, so I hide myself.

I didn't even know that's what I was doing -- have been doing -- all this time.

'Cause, what do you mean, "I hide," right? Who am I? Have you ever met me? Dean Winchester doesn't hide.

I hide myself so well that most people don't see me, and sometimes, I resent them for that. Even though that's what I'm showing them.

I hide myself so well that most of the times, I don't even see myself. Even I think that's just it. That's me.

Some people do see me. And it's so easy to fall in love with them. Though I don't know if it's really love. I'd like to call it that anyway, 'cause that's an easy term to cover so many feelings. I can't name all of them. I'm bad with feelings, even worse with naming them, giving them voices, telling them apart. They are just that, *feelings*. What more do you want me to say?

I struggle so bad with those therapists' questions about "How do you feel," ever since Sammy got enough of my shit, dragged my ass out of the bunker, and dumped me at the shrinks' places. Like how Dad used to say what people suggested he do after Mom. But that's not the same. I know Sammy didn't think I went crazy like those people thought Dad did. But the point is, that doesn't do anything. Nothing ever will, again. I just know that.

But I figure it doesn't hurt to do a little bit of journaling. Dad kept that journal all these years. Every year on their wedding anniversary, he wrote down which anniversary that would have been: iron, wool and copper, bronze, all the way till the last, till he left the journal with me. I never got the chance to ask how that felt. I don't have to now. I don't want to know.

I don't even have a day that's anything like an anniversary to remember for us. We don't have anything like that, like what Mom and Dad had. We don't have anything.

I don't know if it's better or worse.

Dad's last is the 27th.

And 23 of them, Mom wasn't there.

I can't imagine how I can pass this many years by myself, without you. I never thought to ask Dad how he did that, either.

I don't think I could, not really. But you'd want me to, that's what Sammy said (Yeah, he knew now). So I'll try. I'm trying, alright? I'm... I'm really trying so damn hard, Cas, you bastard. For you. But you're not here. You... you --

[Many unrecognizable scribbles and scratched-out lines]

Sorry, I... I had to take a moment. I...

Where was I? Oh, right. Shrinks, and feelings. Not my best suit, but you already knew that. But I just got a few new realizations you probably didn't know.

So. I got feelings. And I thought, well, I don't really know what those feelings are, and I don't really know what love is, either, but so many people enjoy talking about love, and it sounds like love can mean so many different things, can cover so many different feelings, then I couldn't get it wrong to name my feelings that too, right? At least some of those feelings gotta get covered by that magic word, right? So that's how I used that word, "love." I told Cassie that when I thought we were serious and this thing between us would last; I told Lisa that when I thought I'd settle down with her for the rest of my life and what we had back then would last. I thought they see me -- I really did, that young me -- in a way others don't, and that alone is enough for me to give a pause on everything else, on hunting, on what Dad might want me to do, on what Sammy needed me to do, for once I'd want to just live for myself for a bit, stay and feel that just a little bit longer, that feeling of there's someone out there who really sees me, so I stayed at their doorsteps and hoped. For a moment, they said the love back too -- but that's before they really see it, see what I really am, what it is that they signed up for by mistake, and then, there is nothing.

I don't blame them. I don't blame anyone. Hell, who would ever really want to see this mess, you know? How naive I gotta be to imagine someone would ever *want* to get anywhere close to this mess that's called Dean Winchester, right?

So I hide myself even better. Put out an even better mask out front (That's what the shrinks called it, in case you wonder how I get that idea). At least I thought I did.

Then, there's you.

You see me in a way no one else ever did. You look at me and you see my soul. And I'm not talking about the angelic laser eye superpower. Just the way you are. You see me, I know you do -- I know now, before I understand what it is. I should have known earlier, way earlier.

But you always can see me, whether I know it or not. You see it when I'm not so hot, when I'm trying to bury all my fear and worry, back then when I had the mark, or when I got an archangel dick bumping in my head, or so many other times. I can put on the mask before anyone else, build a castle inside my head, put up freaking high walls around it and dig up the deepest trench in front of it, and stay inside -- and everyone else might get convinced for a hot minute that I'm fine and tough, but not you. I can never hide anything like that from you. Never before you. You'd ask me if I'm alright, 'cause you'd already know I'm not. You're the only one who knows how scared I was all those times, and you're the only one I can actually admit that and talk about that, talk about how I feel without cringing, 'cause you'd just be there, look at me and hear me, that's all. And that's enough. That's all I really needed.

I never realized how much I need that -- need you. I don't need solutions or fixes or help -- I could use them, but not really what I *need*. I just need that, what you give me, what you are, and you're all that I can ask for, all that I ever need. How can I never realize how much you hear me and see me? I only wish I could be the same for you. I really, really wish I could too.

But here's something you don't know. The way I think of those feelings when I got them, when I see your eyes drilling a hole in me, when you chose me over anything else, over and over again, I used that word in my head -- "love." I think it is that. Do you know that? When I told you to shove your angelic peace and lord's plan up your lily-white ass twelve years ago, and you looked back at me with burning fire in your eyes and jumped in front of me to hold off those bigger dicks from upstairs, I remember, to this day, thinking, *Shit, I think I'd be in love with this crazy son of a bitch if we make it*. And then you came back, all pissed and snarky, but you'd still indulge me to go to that ridiculous brothel and smile with me; right there when I was laughing my ass off, I remember thinking, *God, he's so weird* and *I think I just love him a little bit more now*. And then one thing after another, on and on, *a little bit more* and *a little bit more*.

But the other part of me also thinks that just can't be right. And by whatever social norm we grew up with, I just always think I'm not in any position to use that word, with you. Not out loud, anyway. We're not anything like my Mom and Dad, right? Or me and Cassie. Or me and Lisa. We are just... not that, and can never be. You're my friend, my best friend, but it's not like we're in a relationship or anything, or ever will be. I'm the billboard example and dictionary definition of a bad relationship anyway, and I wouldn't ever want to risk losing whatever we already had for what we can never have. And you're an angel, for Christ's sake. How can I even *dream* about using that human word on you? *Love* you? Isn't that a blasphemy? I know I usually don't care about that, curse like a storm to the gods and God himself and I don't give a rat's ass, but not when it comes to you. That's why, for all these years, I lost count of how many millions of times it has happened, but whenever I want to say "I love you," I stop myself and I say "Thank you," I say "I need you," I say "You are family." Because it's safer, it's more appropriate to use between friends and family. It's what friends would use normally. There's no risk of ruining anything.

You never responded to those words in any way other than taking them for what they are. As if they really are just the gratefulness and appreciation that I can offer generously but you don't need any more. You never said anything more than just a proper response to that. But now I suspect... I realize, you must know -- you had to know. If you see me as well as I think you do, if the angels can pick up longings like I know you do, then you must know, what those words were before I switched them. But you don't point it out, you never say anything. Because you can't. You are not in any position to, either, just like me. Because as long as I hide, you're letting me. Because you know me so well, that you wouldn't do anything that makes me uncomfortable. But I think I can still feel it, and you can feel it, so whatever it is between us, that's what it is, and that's good enough for us. Or so I thought.

Even after so many years, so often, I'd still get a sudden startle at how well you know me, how much you see me. No one was ever like that for me. You see me so thoroughly, and you still chose to stay... it shouldn't be possible. It never ceases to amaze me that you're here at all, still here after everything. Do you know there was this one time -- you weren't around at that time, but we got into a witch case and I got cursed (of course I did). I was about to forget my name and possibly dying (scratch possibly but don't worry about it). So I asked Sammy to give me a CliffsNotes about my life so that even when I forgot how to breathe, I'd still try really really hard with my cursed head to remember these last little things that make me me. And Sammy gave me four names, my own, my brother, my mom, and you -- because you're my best friend. I was so unbelievably thrilled to know I got a best friend even as I was dying, because I never had one; and immediately so devastated, because I forgot your name just seconds later -- I thought I lost you, my memory lost you. I swore I'd never lose you again, or forget you, 'cause the pain of that was unbearable -- and I knew that all too well now... I don't know what to do about it, Cas. It seems, I never know what to do about you, so I never did anything. I think, maybe I didn't want anything to change. I mean, what more can I ask?

Out of all the people I've ever met, I've ever known, including my immediate family, people who I thought would be closer, people who went through something like what I went through and who I thought would be easier to know me and see me, people who were born and grew up in the same soil and dirt that I did, people who are almost same as me, hunters, *humans*. Out of all these people, you're the one who sees me the best. You're not even from around here. Guess that's just how this works, you'd just never know who'd see you. Whether you let them or not. How well you hide or pretend. If they see you, then they see you, and there's no hiding before them. I'd forever be naked before you -- figuratively speaking, of course. I'm so goddamn lucky to have one -- to have you. Even though I know -- I thought I knew -- I can never have you in any more ways than we already had. I just always thought, that's all we're ever gonna be: friends. Best friends. And really, I thought, that's the best I can ask for and I ain't complaining.

But maybe, secretly, selfishly, discontentedly, I still want more. But I can't. I can't let myself. I want more people to see me the way you do. But I also want no one to see me the way you see. I guess I'm scared. It's scary to be seen. There's way too many nasty bits in me. It's like getting naked in public -- not that I'm scared of getting naked, but you get what I mean. You'd have to trust them enough to be comfortable with being seen. Know you'd be accepted as you are without pretense, know you won't be judged, know you'd just be *seen* and that's all that matters. You don't even need to do *anything*. You just need to be there, and you just need to *see* me -- and not run away after that. But that's the easiest and the most difficult thing to do. And you nailed it. I have no idea how you even did that.

You must think it's that "I love you" that hit me the hardest, back there, don't you? And, yeah, it shot me in the heart so bad I can't breathe, I can't move. But that's not even remotely the worst, you know? It's the part before that. The part when you just look at me like I'm anything beautiful, anything worth keeping your angelic eyes on and not moving away for a second, the part when you say how you see me. I hide myself since the first day the bad things happened, I hide myself from Dad, from Sammy, from myself, from the world, 'cause that's the only way I gotta survive everything. For forty years I hide and I pretend, the same way I've always lowered my voice so I could be more like Dad, and I'm fine -- I thought I was fine. And there you are, standing there, looking at me, really looking at me, really seeing me. And tell me you see me. You see the real me. You see me for who I am and you are still, *still*, here, loving me, ready to do everything, every fucking stupid thing, for me. What am I gonna do about that? How am I supposed to not break from that? The only person in the world who can see me like that is gone. Do you have any idea what you have really done?

The way you see me, the way you see the good in me, the little part that's not broken, the way you see through all the anger and hurt, and you just, simply, look me in the eyes and see me. And there I was, being seen -- it's like becoming real for the first time, being born again, being naked before you, being out of the cocoon for the first time, being able to breathe the real air for the first time, being scared shitless, 'cause that's the real me behind this face and mask really feels. I froze. I was scared. I was freed. It's like the sun shines on me for the first time behind a wall I built for myself long, long ago, and you're the first to reach there. Like ashes on an old table that no one touched for a long time finally get wiped. But then, you're gone. The sun was just there for a split second, and I'm thrown right back into the ultimate coldness and darkness. How could I survive this coldness and darkness after I know how it feels to have the sunshine on my face? Your smile, your tears, the way you look at me, the way you always have been looking at me for the past twelve years. How could I forget that now? How could you ask me to live in a world without you in it now? How could I think of any of our memories any other way, know what we could have but never did, and survive that now? How could I see myself again without you being there, seeing me? Cas, tell me, how could I? Come back and tell me... Please... I need you...

[Many, many pages later]

Jesus Christ, I know it. I know you'd be back. You always do. Of course you'd come back. Of course you hear me again wherever you are. I don't think I need this journal now. You bastard. Don't do that again.
