Love has never been the problem. And love has never been the answer.

I've been loved. And it's always the love that hurt me the most. Hurt and pain, in the name of the love.

Sometimes I wonder, if things would be easier if the love is just not there. Not so overwhelming. Not like that.

I'm never loved in the way I want to be loved. Even though I don't know what that is like.

I yearned for love, but it's not love that I look for.

The one love, that I ache so much for, that I got so comfortable with, is the one case that love would never be said. It would never be heard. It would have no chance. But I know so certain that it's there. And that is the thing I wanted. It's love that is not. It didn't hurt. Because it's not what it claimed to be.

And that is my "one thing I want, is something I know I can't have."

I endure it. And I yearn it. But I'm content with how it is. And I'm hollow and empty without it. All at the same time. It has been five years. It might as well have been ten years. It might be so so much longer. And it might last until the end of the life. And so be it.

I understand him so so well now. Dean Winchester.

Love has never really been the thing missing from his life. He knows people in his life love him. He knows they do things in the name of love. But that doesn't mean the hurt is okay. That doesn't mean that's what he wanted or needed.

His father loved him, and his messed-up childhood is all because of that. So many other people have loved him, but that doesn't stop them from leaving them. And now, Cas says he loves him. And now he will have to die and leave him too. Love isn't the most important thing. He knows he's loved. But everything else is not automatically right when there is love. He can't answer, he can't do anything, certainly not to reciprocate, because love doesn't mean anything if it's followed by loss, by leaving, by pain and hurt. It just, wouldn't make anything right. It just hurt more. Hurt so much. Too much.

It's not love that he needs. It's the stay, the company, the listening, the hanging out, the understanding, the support, the "being there" that matters.

So Cas is right, actually. It's not in the having, it's in the being. It's about being there for him and making him comfortable and happy, not dying, not leaving, not scared. But it's so so difficult to do for them. And it's certainly not about "being" for just ten seconds.

Not meant to die but by doing so, Cas doesn't know how much he has hurt Dean again, and probably even the worst of all. And the "I love your" just made it so so much worse. It just proved to Dean again and again that love doesn't matter, love doesn't mean anything, love can just lead straight to leaving, to more hurt and pain, to the loss of love, and he can't do anything about it.

Cas, you sacrificed yourself to save Dean, but you also destroyed his world. Would you possibly be happy if you'd ever known how much pain and hurt you just caused Dean? All this time you want to protect him from any harm, sacrificing yourself to protect him, in the end, it is this very thing, the sacrifice, the loss of you, that's gonna hurt the most. He can endure Apocalypse and end of universe just fine, but he can't handle losing you. And you don't know that. Because you're already gone.

<br>

Please stop loving me. I think, as if I'm the one and same as Dean. It's not love that I want or feel short of. Love is the cause of all the problem and hurt and harm and pain and ache. Then please, just stop. Don't love me, and don't hurt me anymore.
