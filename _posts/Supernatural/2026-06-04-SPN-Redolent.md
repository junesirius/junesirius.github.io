just learned a beautiful word from Merriam-Webster's word of the day: "redolent," and it has a very beautiful example sentence: "The late-spring meadow was redolent of wildflowers and petrichor." And I'm falling in love with the word "petrichor" too.

And that reminds me, of all the Alpha/Beta/Omega stories of Destiel fic that I've read (or those that are not ABO but also have smell descriptions), Cas's smell is almost always described as something like ozone, sky, storm, fresh air, occasionally something vegetation-related, like pines or grass. But never once have I read something like what's in that beautiful example sentence: "He was redolent of wildflowers and petrichor." It's close enough to the smell of storm or ozone and coming from sky, but "petrichor"! So much more down to Earth and soil. I imagine his smell will change too as he starts falling and changing, getting closer to Earth, figuratively and literally. Dean might smell the sky from him when they first met, and then rain and storm, then petrichor, soil and grass, and honey, even just the smell itself could tell him how much closer Cas gets to him, falls for him, how much he loves him and allows himself to feel the love.
