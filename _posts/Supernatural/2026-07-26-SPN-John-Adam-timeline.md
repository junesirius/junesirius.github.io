Jesus Christ, I've never cross-checked Adam's story timeline with Sam/Dean's, and now I'm so, so pissed about John (so much more than I already was, from all the A+ parenting).

So John met Adam's mom on January 9, 1990, on the ghoul case, having his "not a monk" life. That's when Dean was 11 and Sam was 7. And you know what else happened around that time? Dean made his first sawed-off shotgun in sixth grade (which was probably just the same year or the year before that) (which John kept it forever in his storage). And the Shtriga case that almost killed Sam, that was "sixteen or seventeen years ago" from 2006, making it right around 1989 or 1990.

Adam was born on September 29, 1990; he didn't have any contact with John until he was twelve -- until the year 2002. This is the year when Dean's 23 and Sam's 19, the Stanford era. Which means when Dean's solo hunting, and Sam doesn't want to talk with either Dean or John, what's John doing? Taking Adam to ball games on his birthday, September 29, 2002; teaching him pool and poker. Imagine there's a case in September around that time, and Dean says to John they should go investigate together, cover more ground, watch out for each other's backs. And John goes, "No, I can't, I need to go somewhere else; it's on you now, Dean." And he drives to Minnesota and takes Adam to watch games, because it's his birthday.

John bought Adam his first beer when he was 15 and taught him to drive in the Impala. That's the year 2005. And oh boy, don't we know enough about what 2005 was like for Sam and Dean. The year of "Dad's on a hunting trip and he hasn't been home in a few days". Guess what, his "hunting trip" includes driving all the way to Minnesota to buy Adam his first beer and teach him to drive in the Impala. John wouldn't be able to drive the Impala to Adam's 15th birthday now (because Dean's driving it to go find Sam in Stanford, on September 13, 2005), but he can still drive his truck to Minnesota on September 29, 2005. And you want to know what Sam/Dean is doing at that time? September 27, 2005, time of "Dead in the Water", Dean almost drowned saving a kid from a vengeful spirit in the lake. And he doesn't pick up his phone when Dean calls him, crying, needing his father's help.

Wow, clapping. What a father of the year you really are, John Winchester.
