Oh god I had a thought and it's making me sick. Dean said it himself:

> "Just 'cause you love someone doesn't mean you should stick around and screw up their life. So I can't be here."

What if Cas has read his mind or memory and heard the same line, and ever since then, this has been his philosophy whenever he wants to stay. He knows he loves Dean, and he wants to stay, he really does, but then he remembers Dean saying, "Just 'cause you love someone doesn't mean you should stick around and screw up their life." And he believes he's screwing up their life. So he leaves. Every time he wants to stay, he thinks that, so every time he leaves.

And every time Cas leaves, Dean becomes a wreck.

Not even talking about any of the times when Cas is dead, just every time when Cas leaves, flying away or walking away, that immediate second when Dean realizes the space Cas occupied a second ago becomes empty, his face does something. You can just tell the exact moment when he realizes Cas has left.

There's so much pain and anger and loss and yearning, right there, but he can't say any of that. He just reflexively calls the angel's name, hoping Cas would answer, and his eyes automatically search the entire room or their surroundings, hoping Cas maybe just moved to a different corner, but it's always empty.

When there's someone there witnessing, he'd jokingly comment something like "Friggin' angel," or how much Cas sucks at saying goodbye -- because humor has always been his coping mechanism -- but even the joking face would flatten and still at that very instant when he realizes Cas just left him, again.

And when there's no one watching, that's when the emotions really get overwhelming. He'd just get soaked in his own feelings. What's right there written on his face every time, is how much he misses Cas and wants him to stay and hates that he leaves, but he couldn't say any of that.

So he just suffers and endures and suppresses, all this time.

And Cas doesn't know any of that. Cas doesn't know what Dean's like whenever he's away.
