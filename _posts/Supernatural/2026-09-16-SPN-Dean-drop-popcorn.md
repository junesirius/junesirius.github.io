I feel Dean is the kind of person who would drop a popcorn, and then do an acrobatic move to catch it right before it falls to the ground, and look around really proudly, showing off the popcorn he just caught, and be like, "Did you see that?"

Sam would snort and roll his eyes and laugh at him being ridiculous or childish (in the sibling way).

And Cas would smile fondly and nod and say he saw that and Dean had shown great athletic nimbleness, which would just make Dean more pleased and satisfied with himself before he ate that popcorn.

And Sam would look between the other two back and forth and laugh at both of them for being ridiculously similar to a married couple (in a very fond/happy for them kind of way). (He's not wrong.)
