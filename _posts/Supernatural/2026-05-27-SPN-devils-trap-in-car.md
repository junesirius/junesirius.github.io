made a devil's trap for my Baby's trunk, and holy crap does it look awesome

hidden just perfectly under the mat, just like how they used to like to paint under the carpet (and ruined Crowley's favorite pricy rug)

made it with ripstop nylon fabric and nylon tape, and damn I love that tape's texture and color (I'd definitely get more of that after using up what I got for this little art project), and it's strong and thin and lightweighted and waterproof and tear-proof (which is important, right? just in case demons trapped in the truck start struggling and tear apart the trap to escape)
