(inspired by [tumblr post](https://www.tumblr.com/castiel--for--king/823652059851145216) about how it's an interesting mechanism that angels can't do anything if you just trap them in holy fire, looks like God's backdoor safety measure to make sure angels behave. To think about that, it's also too easy to summon or banish an angel, even easier than doing that to demons.)

<br>

If you want an angel to come, you can summon them.

If you want an angel to leave, you can banish them.

If you want an angel to never leave again, you can trap them in holy fire.

Humans have so many ways to control an angel if they want.

But Dean Winchester doesn't need to do any of that.

He just needs to look at the sky and think, *please help me*. And his angel will come.

He just doesn't know all he has to say is, *please don't leave*. Then his angel will stay.
