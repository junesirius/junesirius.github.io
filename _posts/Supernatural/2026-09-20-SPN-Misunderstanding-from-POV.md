Where are there so many misunderstandings around Dean?

Because so many people see Dean from Sam's perspective, because the show almost always shows us the Dean in Sam's perspective, so frequent and so natural that we even forget this is actually not the factual reality, it's just Sam's perspective.

But Sam doesn't know Dean as well as he thinks he does. He's not always right, not about so many things about Dean. And the viewers sometimes just don't realize that. And here're some proof:

<br>

Why do people often think Dean can't cook?

Because Sam thinks Dean can't cook.

[TBA snapshot where Dean homecooks really good burger]

Sam: "I didn't know you know what a kitchen is."

Dean raised you up and prepared for your breakfast and lunch and dinner when John isn't there. He isn't just pouring your cereal all this time. You really he can do that without knowing how to cook?

As soon as Dean gets to live in a place with real kitchen, he puts on apron and prepares the burgers and cooks the meals. But people still don't remember or don't think he can and he loves cooking.

<br>

Why do people think Dean don't read?

Because Sam thinks Dean don't read.

[TBA snapshot where Charlie watch GOT with them]

Sam: "Yes, I love reading, the ones without pictures."

[TBA snapshot where Dean does research and read piles of books in S01; where Dean ask Slaughterhouse-five Vonnegut or Cat's Cradle Vonnegut when Chuck mentions Vonnegut, Sam's surprised he knows them]

Dean does actual reading and he does researches. Sometimes he jokes about it, sometimes he jokingly complains about it. But he's not a blunt, and he reads. He reads for fun (like the Vonnegut novels) and he reads for research (how else would he be able to figure out the cases when he was hunting alone after his twenties during Stanford era?) He reads everything in John's journal, too, memorizing everything and being so familiar with it in the way Sam never gets.

But people still don't think he reads, and think all he reads is just porn magazines. People inside that world and people outside alike. They just see him the way Sam see him, remember him the way Sam remember him.

<br>

Why do people think Dean isn't good with kids?

Because Sam thinks Dean isn't good with kids.

[TBA snapshot S01E02 with Lucas, Dean: "I like kids" Sam: "Name three kids you know"; Dean with Bobby John, Sam surprised]

Sam mocks him for saying he likes kids. It's Sam who thinks Dean isn't good with kids. But Dean has always been so good with kids, he likes them, knows how to get to them, and kids love him and trust him so easily. There's Lucas, Bobby John, Ben, Emma, Claire, Krissy, Jesse, Jack.

But people still think he doesn't know how to be around kids. Because he doesn't have a kid of his own to shut them up. He doesn't get a chance to.

<br>

Sometimes, even Dean sees himself the way Sam sees him, the way the world and everyone else sees him. But that's not who he really is.

He's loving and caring; he cooks for his family. He's smart and educated; he reads real books for research and for entertainment. He's compassionate and fatherly; he comforts kids and knows the language to talk to them.

But the world and the viewers forget about them so often.

The only one doesn't forget is Cas. The only one sees him different from the way the world and himself sees him is Cas. And he makes sure Dean hears that and remembers that, even when that's the last thing he gets to tell him, so that he may see himself the way the person who loves him sees him.
