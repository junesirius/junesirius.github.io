My midnight "a cent for your thought" realization: "I see you" is way more romantic than "I love you".

<br>

<hr style="border: 1.5px solid; color: #36165a; opacity: 0.8;">
<br>

I'm thinking about your words that night, again. And I play them in my head, over and over again. I'm afraid once I stop, I'll forget them, I'll forget you -- I'll forget the way your voice tremble, the way your eyes sparkle but still staring at me without blinking, the way you smile, the way you cry. That's the only thing I have now. I don't even have a trench coat to take with me or to burn this time. I have nothing.

I'm thinking about what you said, one word at a time. I wrote them down long ago. I don't want to get them wrong, not any single word you said. But what does that matter? I look at them, I write them again and rewrite them again, but so many things still don't make sense, not in my head, anyway. Not the way I see myself, just like you said, man, I need you here telling me those things when I can't see them myself. I -- I need you here.

I don't need people to love me. Not really.

I need people to see me.

But I'm so afraid for people to see me, so I hide myself.

I didn't even know that's what I was doing -- have been doing -- all this time.

'Cause, what do you mean, "I hide," right? Who am I? Have you ever met me? I don't hide.

I hide myself so well that most people don't see me, and sometimes, I resent them for that. Even though that's what I'm showing them.

I hide myself so well that most of the times, I don't even see myself. Even I thought that's just it. That's me.

Some people do see me. And it's so easy to fall in love with them. Though I don't know if it's really love. I'd like to call it that anyway, 'cause that's an easy term to cover so many feelings. I can't name all of them. I'm bad with feelings, even worse with naming them, giving them voice, telling them apart. They are just that, *feelings*. What more do you want me to say?

I struggle so bad with those therapists' question about "How do you feel," ever since Sammy got enough of my shit, dragged my ass out of bunker and dumped me at the shrinks' places. Like how Dad used to say what people suggested him to do after Mom. But that's not the same. I know Sammy didn't think I went crazy like those people thought Dad did. But point is, that doesn't do anything. Nothing ever will, again. I just know that.

But I figure it doesn't hurt to do a little bit journaling. Dad kept that journal all these years. Every year in their wedding anniversary, he wrote down which anniversary that would have been, iron, wool and copper, bronze, all the way till the last, till he left the journal with me. I never got chance to ask how does that feel. I don't have to now. I don't want to know.

I don't even have a day that's anything like an anniversary to remember for us. We don't have anything like that, like what Mom and Dad had.

I don't know if it's better or worse.

Dad's last is 27th.

I can't imagine how I can pass this many years by myself. I never thought to ask Dad how he did that, either.

I don't think I could, not really. But you'd want me to, that's what Sammy said. So I'll try. I'm trying, alright? I'm... I'm really trying so damn hard, Cas, you bastard. For you. But you're not here. You --

[Many unrecognizable scribbles and scratched-out lines]

Sorry, I... I had to take a moment. I...

Where was I? Oh, right. Shrinks, and feelings. Not my best suit, but you already knew that. But I just got a few new realizations you probably didn't know.

So. I got feelings. And I thought, well, I don't really know what those feelings are, and I don't really know what love is, either, but so many people enjoy talking about love, and it sounds like love can mean so many different things, can cover so many different feelings, then I couldn't get it wrong to name my feelings that too, right? At least some of those feelings gotta get covered by that word, right? So that's how I used that word, "love." I told Cassie that when I thought we were serious and this thing between us would last; I told Lisa that when I thought I'd settle down and what we had back then would last. I thought they see me -- I really did, that young me -- in a way others don't, so I stayed at their doorsteps and hoped. For a moment, they said the love back too -- before they really see it, and then, there is nothing.

I don't blame them. I don't blame anyone. Hell, who would ever really want to see this mess, you know? How naive I gotta be to imagine someone would ever *want* to get anywhere close to this mess that's called Dean Winchester, right?

So I hide even better. At least I thought I did.

Then, there's you.

You see me in a way no one else ever did. You look at me and you see my soul. And I'm not talking about angel laser eye shit. Just the way you are. You see me, I know you do -- I know now, before I understand what it is. I should have known earlier, way earlier.

But here's something you don't know. The way I think of those feelings when I got them, when I see your eyes drilling a hole in me, when you chose me over anything else, I used that word too, "love." I think it is that. But the other part of me also think that just can't be right. And by whatever socially acceptable standard we grew up with, I just always think I'm not in any position in using that word, with you. We're not like my Mom and Dad, right? Or me and Cassie. Or me and Lisa. We are just... not that. You're my friend, my best friend, but it's not like we're in a relationship. So whenever I want to say "I love you," I stop myself and I say "Thank you." Because it's safer, it's more appropriate between friends, it's what people would use normally.

You never responded to those words in any way other than taking them for what they are. As if they really are just the gratefulness that I can offer generously but you don't need any. You never said anything more than just a proper response for that. But now I suspect... I realize, you must know, you had to know. If you see me as well as I think you do, then you must know, what those words were before I replaced them. But you doesn't say it. Because you can't. You are not in any position to, either; just like me. Because as long as I hide, you're letting me. Because you know me so well, that you wouldn't do anything that makes me uncomfortable. But I think I can still feel it, and you can feel it, so whatever it is between us, that's good enough for us. Or so I thought.

You know me too well. You see me. It's such a surprise. Out of all these people I knew, people I grew up with, people who I thought would be closer, people who went through what I went through and I thought would be easier to know me and see me, people who were born and grew in the same soil and dirt that I did, people who are almost same as me, hunters, *humans*. Out of all these people, you're the one who see me the best. Guess one just never know who'd see you. Whether you let them or not. I'm goddamn lucky to have one. To have you. Even though I know -- I thought I know -- I can never really have you. I just always thought, that's all we ever gonna be: friends. Best friends. And really, I thought, that's the best I can ask for.

But maybe, secretly, selfishly, discontentedly, I still want more. But I can't. I want more people see me the way you do. But I also want no one see me the way you see. I guess I'm scared. It's scary to be seen. It's like getting naked -- not that I'm scared of getting naked, but you get what I mean. You'd have to trust them enough to be comfortable with being seen. Know you'd be accepted as you are without pretence, know you won't be judged, know you'd just be *seen* and that's all that matters. You don't even need to do *anything*. You just need to be there, and you just need to *see* me. But that's the easiest and the most difficult thing to do. And you nailed it.

Honestly, it's so much more romantic than "I love you." It's "I see you." It's the most romantic thing one can tell another, and that's what you told me. The way you see me, the way you see the good in me, the way you see through all the anger and hurt, and you just, simply, look me in the eyes and see me. And there I was, being seen, being real, being naked before you, being scared shitless 'cause that's the real me behind this face and mask really feels, frozen, and scared. It's like the Sun shines on me for the first time behind a wall I build myself, and you're the first to reach there. But then, you're gone. The Sun is just there for a split second, and I'm thrown back into the ultimate coldness and darkness. How could I survive this coldness and darkness after how it feels to have the sunshine on my face? Your smile, your tears, the way you look at me, the way you always have been looking at me for the past twelve years. How could I forget that now? How could I think of any of our memory any other way, and know what we could have but never did, and survive that now? Cas, tell me, how could I? Come back and tell me... Please...

I think you are scared too. See, you are not the only one here, when you see me, I see you, too. I bet no one ever told you that, huh?
