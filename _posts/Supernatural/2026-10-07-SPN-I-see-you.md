I don't need people to love me.

I need people to see me.

But I'm so afraid for people to see me, so I hide myself.

I hide myself so well that most people don't see me, and I resent them for that. Even though that's what I'm showing them.

Some people do see me. And it's so easy to fall in love with them. Though I don't know if it's really love. I'd like to call it that anyway, because that's an easy term that can cover so many feelings. But also I'm in no position to use that word, so every time I want to use it, I have to filter it out, replace it with something else.

Most often, whenever I want to say "I love you," I say "Thank you." Just this week, there was a time I really wanted to say "I didn't know I can love you more than I already did but apparently I still could," and it took me a while to think of a replacement, and what I ended up saying is, "I didn't know I can appreciate you more than I already did but apparently I still could." He didn't respond to those words, he never did, I suspect he knows what those words were before I replace them, but he doesn't say it, because he can't, he's not in any position to, either; just like me. But I think I can still feel it, and that's good enough. He knows me too well. He sees me. It's such a surprise. Out of all these people I knew, my friends, people I grew up with, people who I thought would be closer, people who I thought would be easier to know me and see me, people who were born and grew in the same soil that I did, people who knew me for five, or ten, or fifteen, or twenty years. Out of all these people, he's the one who sees me the best. Guess you never know who'd see you. Whether you let them or not. I'm glad I'm lucky to have one. Even if I know I can never really have him. I wouldn't want to anyway. This is the best I can ask for. But maybe secretly, selfishly, uncontentedly, I still want more people like him, more people who can see me like he can. But I'd guess I'd be scared too. It's scary to be seen. It's like getting naked. You'd have to trust them enough to be comfortable with being seen. Know you'd be accepted as you are without pretence, know you won't be judged, know you'd just be *seen* and that's all that matters. They don't even need to do *anything*. They just need to be there, and they just need to *see* you. But that's the easiest and most difficult thing to do.

That's why "Avatar" also has that famous line that touches me so deeply before I understand why: "I see you." It's so much more than "I love you." It's "I see you." Cas did that to Dean too. In my heart, that's the most romantic thing one can tell another. And Cas did that to Dean.
