Rewatching S06E20 and realizes this episode is so fundamental and revealing in destiel's feelings and their separate POVs (We need Cas POV so desperately awww give me more)

Only *just* realized how deeply in love with Dean Cas (already) was at the end of *Swan Song* (right after he died for Dean *a second time*), and he actually *really* needed Dean at that time. But that's exactly when Dean went to live with Lisa and retired from hunting, and that domestic life looks so wholesome (and that's Sam's death wish) that Cas doesn't think he has any right to disturb, he doesn't think he's needed by Dean anymore in Dean's new *normal* life. Like S15E03, Cas walked out (flied out, at the end of S05E22), but Dean didn't ask him to stay (also didn't get a chance to, Cas sucked at saying goodbyes).

That's why there's no way for Cas to break back into Dean's life to ask for his help. Cas said "Where are you when I needed you most" and that's absolutely true (to him), because from where he's standing, Dean's off on his own path, his own life, a normal human life with Lisa and Ben, he's settled, and Cas does not exist in that image and should not be. Dean has cut out his connection with hunting and anything supernatural, and, to Cas, that also means angels, that also means him. Dean's new life doesn't have Cas. So yeah, to him, of course Dean's not there for him like he used to be anymore.

But Dean's "I was there, where were you" is also absolutely true (to him), because *we* know how unsettling he was during that year, he was never really settled (as soon as there's any sign of supernatural activity around him, he's back into the life), but *Cas doesn't know that*.

It's like every single miscommunication fic I've read, god.

Cas's whole journey and all (bad) choices in S06 is practically entirely because he loves Dean too much and too deeply, just like he admitted in this very episode, "I did all of this for you, because of you." He has to shoulder everything alone (like he always thought he has to), because he doesn't think he has a place in Dean's life, he doesn't have any position to disturb Dean's life, because he has absolutely no idea how much he matters to Dean (in his mind, he has always thought himself expansible from beginning to the end, and it never changed from S04 to S15, and that himself is worth nothing, means nothing to Dean). And he doesn't believe Dean would ever care about him, at least not as much as he cares about Dean; and of course, Dean would never love him back.

Dean has said "Next to Sam, you and Bobby are the closest thing I have to a family, you're a brother to me, a friend", but just not the way Cas loves him. What does the word "family" and "brother" mean to Cas? S05 beginning, Cas: "I have killed two brothers this morning for you." For Cas, "brothers" are the things he has no problem killing every episode without blinking. Even if he could sense Dean and Sam's brotherhood is tight and close, human brotherhood is different, as an angel, he just couldn't possibly understand the depth of how much connection and emotions Dean wanted to say, wanted him to understand. So when Dean told him "you're a brother", Dean means "you matter so much to me, I could die for you", and Cas heard "you matter nothing and you're replaceable, I could kill you for something more important." Sweet Jesus, talking about miscommunication. And Dean's been telling him "You're like a brother to me" basically all twelve years. And neither of them ever really clarify the meaning of it... Dean doesn't realize he needs to, and Cas thinks he understands just the way he needs (because it fits his self-image of un-importance)... Those idiots.

So that's what pushed Cas away even further, moving to retort: "What are you gonna do to me? You're just a man, I'm an angel." Which is exactly what he used to do to his brothers that he doesn't agree with: he kills them. So his intuitive thought and expectation of what Dean would do, is that Dean might try to kill him. But then his primitive built-in angel arrogance (of being a superior species than human) kicks in, reminding him humans can't kill angels easily (even though Dean has proved him wrong so many times, Dean has killed angels before, Cas's own ex-supervisor Zachariah included).

Which of course makes Dean even more angry and sad and hurt ("You're just a man" is so belittling and hurtful even take out the context, but Cas might think he's just stating the fact), which leads to "I take down quite some big guys", trying to remind Cas not to underestimate him as a human. But from Cas's POV, that's saying "I have killed big guys, so I can kill you too." Which further proved his impression and self-image and Dean's thought about him. Which practically officially declared them enemies, no matter ho wmuch both of them not want it to be like this.

Neither of them are wrong, they're just looking at things different, standing at different position. Loving a different species (that speaks a different language) turns out to be really fucking difficult.

And I think Dean only realizes Cas really does love him and is in love with him after Cas's S07E02 death. Cas tried so, so hard to make things right, not even caring about his own death, if that means making things right again with Dean. I think that's why the whole S07 Dean's so sick with feelings (the only time his mind is off-hook is when he's poisoned by Leviathan goo burger and becomes all "I don't even care about Cas anymore"). Leading to S08's one-year of mutual penance, and the "pray to you every night" becoming so natural and raw (in the purest place of all the realms where he can finally let his feelings come out raw and blunt).

For some reason the past me has always thought the real "in love" feeling thing doesn't come until after S08, especially because of S05 Cas's "I don't have as much faith in you as Sam" (lack of faith in an angel is kinda a big deal, and if he can't have enough faith in Dean, I wasn't convinced we can talk about love yet), but then S06E20 proved me wrong. Maybe this does start happening at least by the end of S05, so maybe exactly as he realized Dean would do things right, he really could have all the faith in Dean, and that's when he really started falling (from Heaven and in love).

God I really need more Cas POV episodes and stories.
