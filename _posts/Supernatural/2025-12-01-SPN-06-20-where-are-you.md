Rewatching S06E20 and realizing this episode is so fundamental and revealing in destiel's feelings and their separate POVs (We need Cas POV so desperately awww, give me more)

Only *just* realized how deeply in love with Dean Cas already was at the end of *Swan Song* (right after he died for Dean *a second time*), and he actually *really* needed Dean at that time. But that's exactly when Dean went to live with Lisa and retired from hunting, and that domestic life looks so wholesome (and that's Sam's death wish) that Cas doesn't think he has any right to disturb, he doesn't think he's needed by Dean anymore in Dean's new *normal* life. Like S15E03, Cas walked out (flew out, at the end of S05E22), but Dean didn't ask him to stay (also didn't get a chance to; Cas sucked at saying goodbyes).

That's why there's no way for Cas to break back into Dean's life to ask for his help. Cas's "Where were you when I needed to hear it?" is absolutely true (to him), because from where he's standing, Dean's off on his own path, his own life, a normal human life with Lisa and Ben, he's settled, and Cas does not exist in that image and should not be. Dean has cut out his connection with hunting and anything supernatural, and, to Cas, that also means angels, that also means him. Dean's new life doesn't have Cas. So yeah, to him, of course Dean's not there for him like he used to be anymore.

But Dean's "I was there, where were you" is also absolutely true (to him), because *we* know how unsettling he was during that year; he was never really settled (as soon as there's any sign of supernatural activity around him, he's back into the life), but *Cas doesn't know that*.

It's like every single miscommunication fic I've read, god.

Cas's whole journey and all (bad) choices in S06 are practically entirely because he loves Dean too much and too deeply, just like he admitted in this very episode: "I'm doing this for you. I'm doing this because of you." He has to shoulder everything alone (like he always thought he had to), because he doesn't think he has a place in Dean's life, he doesn't have any position to disturb Dean's life, because he has absolutely no idea how much he matters to Dean (in his mind, he has always thought himself expendable from beginning to the end, and it never changed from S04 to S15, and that himself is worth nothing, means nothing to Dean). And he doesn't believe Dean would ever care about him, at least not as much as he cares about Dean; and of course, Dean would never love him back.

Dean has said, "Next to Sam, you and Bobby are the closest things I have to family -- that you are like a brother to me", but just not the way Cas loves him. What do the words "family" and "brother" mean to Cas? S05E02, Cas: "I killed two angels this week. My brothers. I did all of it, for you." For Cas, "brothers" are the things he has no problem killing every episode without blinking. Even if he could sense Dean and Sam's brotherhood is tight and close, human brotherhood is different; as an angel, he just couldn't possibly truly understand the depth of how much connection and emotion Dean wanted to say by telling him he's a brother to him, how desperate Dean wanted him to understand without really saying what he truly wanted to say. So when Dean told him "you're a brother", Dean means "you matter so much to me, I could die for you", and Cas heard "you matter nothing and you're replaceable, I could kill you for something more important." Sweet Jesus, talking about miscommunication. And Dean's been telling him "You're like a brother to me" basically all twelve years. And neither of them ever really clarifies the meaning of it... Dean doesn't realize he needs to, and Cas thinks he understands just the way he needs (because it fits his self-image of unimportance)... Those idiots.

So that's what pushed Cas away even further, moving to retort: "Or what?" What are you going to do to me? "You're just a man. I'm an angel." Which is exactly what he used to do to his brothers (when he has to stop them, when they have disagreement and he can't persuade them): he kills them. So his intuitive thought and expectation of what Dean would do, is that Dean would try to kill him. But then his primitive built-in angel arrogance (of being a superior species to humans) kicks in, reminding him humans can't kill angels easily (even though Dean has proved him wrong so many times; Dean has killed angels before, Cas's own ex-supervisor Zachariah included).

Which of course makes Dean even more angry and sad and hurt ("You're just a man" is so belittling and hurtful even take out the context, but Cas might think he's just stating the fact), which leads to "I've taken some pretty big fish", trying to remind Cas not to underestimate him as a human, but not really saying anything about killing, more emphasizing on *stopping them*. But from Cas's POV, that's saying "I have killed big guys, so I'm capable of killing you too. That's not a problem." Which further proved his impression and self-image and Dean's thoughts about him. Which practically officially declared them enemies, no matter how much both of them don't want it to be like this.

Neither of them is wrong; they're just looking at things differently, standing at different positions. Loving a different species (that speaks a different language) turns out to be really fucking difficult.

And I think Dean only realizes Cas really does love him and is in love with him after Cas's S07E02 death. Cas tried so, so hard to make things right, not even caring about his own death, if that means making things right again with Dean. I think that's why the whole S07 Dean's so sick with feelings (the only time his mind is off-hook is when he's poisoned by Leviathan goo and becomes all "I don't even care about Cas anymore"). Leading to S08's one-year of mutual penance, and the "pray to you every night" becoming so natural and raw (in the purest place of all the realms where he can finally let his feelings come out raw and outright).

For some reason the past me has always thought the real "in love" feeling thing doesn't come for either of them until after or around S08, especially because of S05 Cas's "I don't have the same faith in you that Sam does" (I still believe lack of faith is kinda a big deal to an angel, like Benny said in S08E07 "Aren't you guys all about faith", and if he can't have enough faith in Dean, I wasn't convinced we can talk about love yet), but then S06E20 proved me wrong. Maybe this does start happening around that time, some time before the very finale of S05, before Cas's second resurrection, maybe exactly as he realized Dean would do things right (S05E21 "You are not the burnt and broken shell of a man that I believed you to be."), he realized Dean is worth his faith, so he really started putting all his faith in Dean (he already did once when S04 he believed Dean could have a chance to stop the Apocalypse so he went ahead rebelling and throwing away everything he believed and everything he was, but the failure might cause some little withdraw and hesitation, but then after everything in S05, he's completely gone for Dean), and that's when he really started falling (from Heaven and in love).

God, I really need more Cas POV episodes and stories.
