Do you think Dean would rewrite his memory of Cas's confession and last sacrifice the same way when they were in Purgatory? Do you think he would rewrite it so he doesn't remember Cas saying love him, and only remembers Cas looking at him crying, remembers Cas saying he was never happy, believing it's his fault Cas was never happy, maybe even it's his fault that Empty got here and took Cas. He would grieve and hate himself and blame himself so much like he did in season 8. And when Cas comes back, Dean's mind gets all twisted and tangled up and at one point lets out desperately saying Cas is something he knows he can't have. And Cas goes like, what are you talking about, you know you have me, you know I love you, I told you. And Dean's like, no you never, you just died miserably and were crying and that's all my fault, I broke your heart and I lost you, like I always do. And Cas realizes and sighs, oh Dean you did it again, of course you rewrote your memory, because it's too painful and you'd always blame yourself for something, let me show you what actually happened. And puts two fingers on Dean's forehead. And Dean relives the Despair moment but as if hearing Cas say I love you for the very first time. Dean stutters and staggers back and can't believe it, "You love me?" And Cas goes, "Yes, Dean, I love you. I hope you can remember it this time," and goes kissing him. And Dean does remember it this way so much better.
