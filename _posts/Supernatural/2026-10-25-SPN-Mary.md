When Mary was burned, that was one month before her 29th birthday. She was even younger than Dean when he gets dragged to Hell.

When the demon killed her mother, possessed her father, and killed her boyfriend, and Mary made the deal to save John, she was barely 19, one year after meeting and dating John. Right around the same age when Sam meets Jessica and falls in love with her and then one year later watches her die. And when John was 19, he just came home from Vietnam.

After Mary dies, she becomes a ghost, and her spirit sacrifices herself to protect the boys. After Bobby dies, he becomes a ghost, and he tries to continue helping the boys until he can't. After John dies and the Hell gate opens, his spirit gets out of Hell to help and protect the boys.

So many parallels in the family.
