I only realized just now, Charlie's dagger in her last moment, is that meant to be a replica of Sting from LOTR? Even the cinematography of this shot reminds me of Two Towers. Charlie loved LOTR so much, and her last moment is just like when the Hobbits are facing the Orcs or any big foul creatures.

She lived the life she always wanted, the one full of magic and adventures, but dammit what about the LOTR ending? She also deserved the peaceful Hobbit ending, but she only got a brutal one... Aww I'm crying again rewatching this episode. At least she's reliving the best Christmas moment with her memory in Heaven now.
