I don't have the story for this yet but I have a sudden visual in my head that almost looks like this:

![](https://cdni.fancaps.net/file/fancaps-movieimages/10125419.jpg)

![](https://cdni.fancaps.net/file/fancaps-movieimages/10125420.jpg)

(from *Pirates of the Caribbean: Dead Man's Chest*)

with Cas being the one fused with a sunken ship or some coral thicket, or with the bottom of the ocean, corals and barnacles and sea creatures growing from inside his body, shrimps and crabs and octopus crawling in and out of his body, his sockets, his mouth, his stomach and brain, seaweeds winding his arms and legs. ~~Maybe~~ Could get quite grotesque. But he's still alive. He's just a creature with a long, long lifespan, staying in the same place for too long that he grows with the surroundings and environment he stays in, becoming a part of the ocean around him. He thought he'd just stand at the bottom of the ocean or near that rusty ship for a bit, observing the ocean, the fish, the coral, just enjoying the view (because this is how he spends this long long life) and he forgot about time and he forgot to move. He didn't even really notice how his body erodes and becomes the bed for other lifeforms, and it doesn't hurt him anyway. Or maybe he noticed, and still didn't give it much thought, or even thinking this is something interesting to observe and experience as well, so he just let the barnacles grow on him, seaweeds growing around him, shrimps and crabs taking his skull as habitats. And he's content being a part of the natural world.

And then one day Dean comes. Maybe Dean is a deep diver, an adventurer who happened to pass this area, or a sailor on a ship in the middle of the storm and drowns into the ocean. Maybe he has consciousness when he runs into this rusty sunken ship (maybe he's a professional diver even, hired to explore the historical treasure or meaning or mystery of this ship or something), maybe he's on the verge of dying himself, and he runs into Cas. It must startle him so much when he realizes in the middle of these corals and seaweeds and barnacles, there's a human (or more like a human-adjacent creature) living in the middle of it. He'd think he's long gone, but then Cas opens his eyes and starts moving and talking. Maybe Cas saves Dean from drowning, maybe Dean saves Cas from this hibernation-kind of dormant status, reminding him to finally remember he's alive (or maybe Cas is also almost dead if he can't remember he's alive, but once reminded, he's alive again) and starts moving again. Maybe it's both and they save each other and get back to the land together.

The way Cas looks must freak people out, but not Dean. Dean's fascinated by the mutual symbiosis Cas gets to have with the sea creatures (maybe Dean is an oceanologist or some type of ocean-related scientist/reseacher who's traveling on the sea when his ship is in a storm and he gets thrown into the sea).

(haven't got idea for what happened next, likely the two getting closer and more familiar; if we go angsty route, likely one or both of them are going to run into some trouble again, people in the world gotta be interested in Cas if they ever heard about the story how he grows with the ocean, and then there must be some greedy bad people among them trying to acquire and hurt him, and Dean'd have to feel guilty and responsible to protect him, but he might get hurt along the way, and then Cas would feel guilty thinking it's his fault and maybe sacrifice himself to protect Dean or leaving Dean is the only way to keep him safe or something of the kind; if we go domestic happy path, likely they'd get to learn each other, there'd still be angsty part about the difference in their lifespan and their fundamental difference from being entirely different species. For a classic stereotypical destiel story, one or both of them would definitely reach a point where they sacrifice for each other to protect them, and the other would suffer and save them back and it goes on and on and on.)

At this point, it starts feeling similar to the one I'm working on right now that I don't want to spoil, but the part that makes this idea fun and different is majorly from that very first visual that just popped into my head, the way Cas looks when he's fused with the sea creatures and corals, and also the way Dean stares and startles when he first finds him like that, and then the way Cas very slowly starts moving away from the other parts that hold him there, as if he's just waking up, life has just began growing and glowing from inside him, life that almost as if it's brought to him by Dean.
