It's happening, goddamn it's happening, I should have known. It's just too easy to get me hooked on something -- something new, or something way, way old. I've been preventing myself from watching anything, just for fear of this happening, but it's happening anyway. And I'd never see it coming until it occupies my entire head. And I'd never guess my next brainrot would be from Wanda&Vision, but unsurprising in a way, since I've always loved them. For ten years, I loved them, and I still care so much about them, and the new shows have been so good. God I'm unsavorable from the Hell made by Marvel and I can't believe I'd do this in 2026, but here it is: I'm rewatching all the Marvel movies with Wanda & Vision in it, just to savor those small sweet moments in the midst of all the tremendous pain and loss. I'll suffer through their death and grief again so that I can relive the joy when they meet again. Which means I'd live through Infinity War and End Game *again*. Really still can't believe I'd do that. Needs to take unbelievable courage to embrace myself for the impact, but what's love if not to face the pain with more bravery? That's how much I love Wanda & Vision, love them enough I'd be willing to let Avengers 3&4 hurt me again, remind me again of all the loss and past glory. Here we go.

### Avengers 2: Age of Ultron

Start right from the very beginning, the one and only, *Age of Ultron*. Also need to get some reminder of the Ultron from ten years ago anyway, seeing him in VisionQuest (and UltronVision), I'd practically forget how he used to be like and his old belief and motivation, so yeah, I'd go from the very beginning, before Vision was even born, before Wanda was Scarlet Witch, before Ultron was Ultron.

I'd forgot it all started with Mind Stone and Loki's scepter, that it's a direct sequel from the very first Avengers, the fight in New York and the first contact of Alien technology, the first time they ever really have the idea of collecting the five stones for the power, even the very first time Thanos ever showing up on the big screen in the post-credit easter egg. So many firsts. Everyone's still so young, so free, so full of jokes and joy. Everyone's still there. God, it hurts so much to see them laugh and have fun and have party and tease and flirt and have feelings and family and each other. Everyone watches everyone's back. They probably don't know that yet, but they're really a family, a way closer and more intimate family than whatever new version or second version or younger version of Avengers would ever be. Like, you couldn't possibly think Dr. Strange and Shangqi and Yelena call each other family, right? Thunderbolts, maybe, but not with the rest of them. They may become comrades, but they're not family. But this very first generation, the six of them, then Wanda and Vision, they are. They really are. Until we lost so many. Until this family is too broken to bear. God, ten years ago really was good old days, the best days, but we didn't know back then.

And I think even the world was in better days, in a more free time, lighter, like a child, without this much bullshit and burden of the current time. That was a time before the pandemic, before the hellish politics, before the hellish AI taking over the world. And they started making movies about AI and joke about AI taking over the world, and guess what, the topic is getting real, so real that they can continue with the debate and fight and still feel innovative and related. Back then, that was still just all ideas and imaginations and science in a fantasy. They can still dream, and they fight, flesh-in-flesh, real beautiful shots and actions and choreography, the type of things that really actually matters without relying so much on the big special effects. That was a time when people still care about real art and can still make real good movies. That was a time when *I* was still young and innocent, fresh into the college, so full of hope and curiosity, without the newly added trauma. God, am I missing the good old movie or the good old time when I was still young and free? Am I missing the good old movie or the friends I went to see these movies with? Nostalgia is killing me, and I'm letting it.

I really forget how much fun they can have around each other, all those jokes and flirts and quibs in the middle of party, in the middle of fight, as if fighting is just a part of fun. When their job isn't to protect the whole universe, when they can still handle their job like an everyday work. Before the big bad became so out of control, before the studio has to up their game and keep uping their game, before things got ridiculously complicated. Things were simple, back then, stories were simpler, people were simpler -- only then can you reach to the feelings, the feelings can be deep, and that's okay, that's always like that.

Watching Cap being still so young, Tony was so young, Banner, Romanoff, Thor, Hawkeye. Everyone. Can't imagine Clint Barton invite any random other "new Avengers" to his safe house where he hid his wife and kids, only this small group, only these people he trusts, only his family meeting his bigger family.

Seeing how it's meant to build up for *Thor: Ragnarök* is nostalgic too. My very first introduction to the universe. The hill that hang me on. One thing led to another, the rest is the history.

Natasha and Bruce still have a thing, feelings. Clint cares for Wanda. Clint quibs with Pietro. They have fun about Mjolnir -- when Mjolnir was still there. Cap and Tony have the little argument. Wanda and Pietro. Wanda and Vision. Vision and Ultron. Tony and Bruce... Everyone's fear in Wanda's induced vision. Everyone's motivation for everything they do: Tony's fear that everyone dying and he's the only one left, because he could have done more but didn't, which is why he'd do more and more every time, as long as he can, he'd do it, just so that his biggest fear doesn't come, as long as he can give, he gave, until he gave his life to save the entire universe, we'd know this is coming since the moment Wanda showed him his biggest fear. Cap's dream with Carter, the lost dance. Thor's vision of Ragnarök, his biggest fear. Natasha's trauma in Red House. Banner losing control to Hulk... Everyone's biggest fear. But there's no real villain.

Wanda and Pietro are scared kids from war, still scared after so many years, after so much things -- all her life, Wanda's losing people she love, people she can't live without, but the world keeps taking them from her, forcing her to live without them. Her parents, then Pietro. Her grief is throughout the entire MCU but overlooked so so easily. She doesn't even get a moment to grieve her brother's death, but she felt it, she felt it so desperately that she's willing to die that day, after killing Ultron, she doesn't even think about how to leave the flying city, she wants to die with her brother, she wants the pain to end. Then Vision found her. Still don't know how Vision knows she's still up there, in the midst of the ruins and corpses of the dead robots. Maybe his super computer brain is doing the headcount, maybe he scanned the ruins one last time for survivors, or maybe he's paying attention to keep tab on Wanda, and he wouldn't have found out if he wasn't. Then there he was, right when Wanda think she'd die, he shows up for her. When Wanda thinks the world abandons her by taking Pietro away from her, Vision comes for her, only for her, and saves her, bringing her to life. She clings to him, before knowing what this is between them, what they can be. She clings to him, because he's there for her when she just lost her brother. In the midst of grief, Vision is there, and that is all the reason to have, all the reason to start with. Vision cares -- when, as a robot, he has no reason to be. Wanda cares -- when, as a traumatized kid, scared and abused and exploited and just experienced the greatest loss she's ever known since losing her parents, she still chooses to walk out of that cave to be a part of Avengers, to save people the way she can. Pietro cares -- and that's what got him killed, that's gonna be what got Wanda and Vision killed too, just different as to when. They care. They care so much it hurts. All of them care so much.

### Captain America: Civil War

Got reminded again how much I love how they love to start a movie: start from the middle of action sequence, start from 30% of the story to lure you in. Also got reminded how much I'd forgotten about them. How I used to be able to replay them all in my head, and now I don't even remember what's the story about or who's in it.

The beginning sequence is almost always just a premise for the real story, same for *Age of Ultron*, same for *Civil War*. The part they fight their way into the hydra place in *Age of Ultron* is only to reveal the Mind Stone (or Loki's scepter, as they knew it back then) and the twins. The fight at the very beginning of *Civil War* with Rumlow (and goddamn am I reminded Rumlow's hot *again*) is just to lead to the Sokovia Accords. I almost even forget how does the whole about Sokovia start, what's the importance of that, everything. God I really do have to rewatch everything (all the important and good ones anyway) before the Doomsday just to keep up, it's been ten years, it's too long. And yeah now I remember it's their home, Wanda and Pietro coming from Sokovia, the first case Wanda and Vision coming to work on, the first save, the first Avengers they're in, that's Sokovia. The city that Ultron made flying into the sky and Tony managed to blow up before it hits the ground and kill half the globe, that's Sokovia. They managed to save everyone on the flying city, but not the city itself. The giant hole in the ground is the proof of all the casualty and loss any of the super hero fights caused -- god, that reminds me, that's still an age when super hero doesn't have to be super human, some of them might, but there are also people like Natasha and Clint, just like ordinary human just with a tad bit of special skills, already good enough to protect the world, but now, now everyone has some weirdass talent or mutant or wicky, making it fancier but so far away from ordinary people, so far away from the reality we'd believe in. Back then, even with giant portal opening to the universe and alien monsters invading and Loki standing on top of New York, we can still believe that's real and that *could* happen, but not now, not anymore.

Miss so much the early interaction between Wanda and Vision. In the quiet moment before the civil war between the team, Wanda goes: "Vis, we talked about this, privacy." when Vision just walks through the wall, reminds me too much of Dean's "Cas, we talked about this, personal space." when Cas stands too close. The little non-human beings trying to learn how to be human and amazed by human beings, while the said human beings that they try to learn from fall in love with their alienness, every little bit of awkwardness becomes cuteness in their eyes, that's where love comes from.
