Just finished VisionQuest finale and I'm so, so, so happy. Haven't been this happy from a show in a million years. God I love it, I love them.

I was literally screaming at the screen when it looks like Vision is going to sacrifice himself to save the world (again). I was literally screaming at the screen: Don't you dare die again. Your boy just found his daddy. Tommy and Billy just found each other. You didn't even know they found each other. Billy is coming. Give him a chance to be with his brother and his father again.

So incredibly happy when the three of them hug. Vision is literally nuzzling his face with both his boys, Tommy first, then Billy. And both boys bury their heads in his shoulder. God, that's the most beautiful thing. The Maximoff family reunion!!

And now, Wanda!! I just always know Wanda wouldn't be dead, the question is just how (and when) she's coming back. And now looks like just the right time! I read it just yesterday I think, that someone predicted Wanda would be on Dr. Doom's team just long enough until she runs into her husband and her boys and she'd flip right then and there.

I wasn't intrigued by any of the trailer-baiting of Doomsday from Marvel over the past year, practically lost all my interest in any of the other old tricks they were trying to use to get us to buy the ticket. Just yesterday, right before the VisionQuest finale, I double-checked with google that neither Vision nor Wanda were confirmed in Doomsday, but now, ha, it's the confirmation and the real temptation. And guess what, I'm weak, I need to be there to see them, the Maximoff family reunion, see the boys meet their mother, see all four of them hug, Wanda is my reason. God I'm weak from Marvel's tricks, and I got the ticket for the first day they'd be on.

But now, twenty minutes later, after cooling down a bit from the exhilaration, I'm starting to feel scared. The only, ***only*** reason Marvel would give us some sweetness, some happiness, is to take it away and make it hurt more. The only reason they'd give us the Maximoff family together, is to take it apart again. I'm so scared for them in Doomsday again. I fear they'd have the briefest moment of happiness, of reunion, only for the mama or papa to sacrifice themselves to protect their sons AGAIN. Or together. Or penance. Something, anything. So many things can go wrong, and they'd lose the family they spent so long to find AGAIN. God, I fear I'd hurt even more with all the hope I have now, with all the joy I'm feeling right now. Not to be a downer on this rare joyful day, but I'm so scared again.

Love is fear. I fear losing them. They'd fear even more losing each other. God, I just feel like I can already see how the evil studio would plot to spare us some tiny little sweetness, some small joy, some relief, some hope before some bigger catastrophe comes in AGAIN, only to crush our hearts again.

<br>

Forget to add: the storytelling is so beautiful too in this series, the way they've been showing us over and over again that painting of the first AI burning because he's feeling too much, and the finale is literally Vision and Ultron burning together because both of them are feeling too much grief, but ultimately because they have two hearts to share the feeling, they eventually didn't burn out. And the scene with all the nanobots burning away, the huge golden cloud of sparks in the air of London, literally exactly matching the fire in the painting, but in this version, this fire isn't to burn down the world with them, but to save the world. God it's masterpiece writing and directing and filming and acting. can't ever praise and love it enough.
