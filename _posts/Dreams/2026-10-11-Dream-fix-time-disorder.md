Many shattered pieces of memory. Not coherent. Can barely form a complete coherent story, but feel like there are some intrinsic logic between them, just that I can't remember, or I lost some (many) important pieces in between.

Remember a long corridor, half inside the building, half connected to the small square opening in the middle of the building. Impression is that this is a teaching building. Maybe a bit like some old middle school building that I can barely remember. It's dim. The corridor is straight and long, connected to another corridor to the right at the end of it. I know my classrooms are on that rightward corridor. Near the end of the corridor, to the left, there's a small window and slightly more space. There are some people sitting on the floor there. Five or so people.

I started at the beginning of the corridor, running toward the end of the corridor, rushing to the classrooms or somewhere. When I past these small group of people, I noticed one of them was old high school classmate Mickee. Probably another one was also another high school classmate Amy, but not as sure. They were pressing their upper left arms with their right hands. Mickee saw me and called out to me, so I paused to talk to him. He asked me if I had vaccine. Only now did I pay attention to the little opening and saw a window there, it was like some old memory of a childhood hospital where you'd take your blood sample from across the window. Apparently it's now the window to take the vaccine (because I had a flu vaccine appointment for next week). I said I didn't. Mickee looked very concerned. I got the feeling that it's not for something like flu, this world had something more dangerous, almost like "Last of Us" or SPN S05E04 Croatoan zombie virus, and not taking vaccine would mean I'd be vulnerable to those zombie virus. I wasn't very concerned, but apparently it was a very serious matter to him and basically everyone in this world.

Cut to another long, straight corridor, but this one was brighter, having more light from all direction, also totally in-door, and feel like it's slightly to the left side (like when shooting, the POV would gradually reveal a new location, and this corridor was revealed from the left, the earlier one was from the right side). There was another person ahead of me, young, female, someone I knew I can trust, but I only see her back and had no idea who she really knows, just knows that she's someone that could give me a sense of safety. I know I had a lot of problem with time, having something like a "Time Traveler's Wife" time traveling disorder problem, that I'd be turning time back and forth too many times and now I can't sync up with the real time, my time would always flow faster or slower than people and the world around me; I can't catch up with the world or the world can't catch up with me. And I suffer from it. I think there were other clips before this that showed how exactly did I suffer, what happened and what were the cost, but I forgot those (almost like I blocked out the bad memories from traumatic experience like I did in real life).

And then here, on this corridor, this was the time when we found a solution. I was listening to or memorizing the instruction as I went through the motion. The instruction was like this: I started from the beginning of this corridor, it's okay if my time at this moment went faster or slower than the rest of the world, and let the person I trust starting from ahead of me, about five to ten steps away. And from there, she started walking slowly ahead, and I try my best to run toward her to meet her. She had a pair of pink slipper in her hand. And when she put the pink slipper on the floor, I should be right there and touch those slippers. If I succeed, that would mean our time got synced at that moment, and from there, my time traveling disorder would get tuned back to normal.

So we started this process, she walked slowly ahead with the pink slipper in her hands, I was about five to ten steps behind her, I knew my watch was going abnormally, but I couldn't remember if it was going backwards or jumping ahead too quick that it would miss the real time. She started walking, I started running. I could see the distance between us shorten, but I was still too far behind. This whole corridor still looked like part of a big teaching building, the walls were white, there were doors here and there. And when she stopped, there was a door to her right. I knew she's about to put the slipper on the floor, but I was still a little too far behind her. I think she couldn't see me or know where exactly I was. She just had to go through those instruction and trust that I could make it. I was a little stressed out/panicky about what if I missed it.

She was very slow in her motion when lowering the slipper to the floor, giving me a lot of time to catch up. When she finally put the pink slipper on the floor, I was three steps away. I couldn't run to the exact position to jump into the slipper (which was what I was supposed to do), so I dived ahead, my whole body pressed to the floor, but my hands managed to touch the slipper the moment it touched the floor. I didn't know if that meant our time was synced or not, this was successful or not, but I think she could see me at that moment, and she saw me on the ground. I considered it a success then.

She walked into that classroom, I walked in after her. It was like an exam happening. We sat at the back of the classroom. I think I was in the fourth column closest to the door, second to the last row, she was in the row behind me, one column to my left. There was a woman sitting near the lecture table, supposed the teacher watching over our exam, but I didn't remember her, don't think she's any of my real teacher from my memory, and I remember thinking I didn't quite like her.

I had my watch folded right before me on my desk. I got the exam paper spread out in front of me, but I was not paying much attention to the papers, mostly to the watch. And I notice the pointer for the second was still jumping ahead and back a lot, every time it was like jumping 15 seconds ahead, and a short while later moving back the time it jumped. I realized the time turbulence around me was possibly still messed up, I still had that time traveling/time turning disorder problem. I turned to her to my left-back side, a little desperate, wanted to tell her I still wasn't synced. But then I think she showed me her watch, was also jumping ahead and back about 15 seconds, and I realized we were synced, that's why she could stably still see me, but we weren't synced with the rest of the world. We wanted to raise attention to the teacher, wanted to say something about the time was wrong, but she didn't pay us much attention, as if we weren't there or weren't standing or talking. Which wasn't very unexpected, knowing our time wasn't synced with the world.

Cut to I got some admission result, and I got two offers from UCSC and UCD, I think the program was something like theater or writing. I was trying to remember what those two school names' full names were, so I was reciting to myself: "UC Santa Clause and UC Davies", and I remember feeling a bit disappointed, because I realized it wasn't UCSD that I thought it was, apparently I confused those two with letters being so similar to UCSD, but they were not what I wanted. I remember thinking those two weren't really good schools for the programs I wanted to go, and then thinking the timing wasn't really good anyway, my original plan would be wait for another five years when the visa problem was finally solved to go, so I decided to not take either of the offers. (Then I woke up naturally, the radio station was playing music in the background, but it wasn't the sound that woke me up, I woke up by myself.)
