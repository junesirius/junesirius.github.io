（一开始的这部分都只有模糊的印象，只是一种对环境和设定的感觉，对实际画面没有很清晰的记忆。）在一个空荡的很暗的房间，天花板很低，整个空间的色调几乎完全是黑白的，方方正正的办公楼角落的感觉，有点荒凉。中间可能有些桌椅，相对这个空间来说看起来太空了。

在离我比较远的一侧有两面墙，都有很大的窗，感觉应该是落地窗（因为天花板很低，所以窗户是从地板到天花板的），感觉窗外看出去的印象好像是在很高的楼里，但实际对所在空间的感觉是好像在地面高度的，所以只是窗户外的看到的有高楼感。虽然有窗，外面天色也是白天，但是仍然感觉整个空间有点昏暗，在我站的地方（离中间桌椅有一段距离，离墙和窗更远）没什么直接的清晰的光线。

我感觉跟别的几个人（可能也就一两个）在一起，虽然人不是很多，但是我有种“we are a team”的团队感，知道我可以信任他们的，我们是一起的、一个团队的。

这个空间/房间外面连接着一条很暗很短的小走廊，再另一边连通一个更大更暗的空间。我们不知道那个空间到底有多大多深。从我站的地方只能看到一个黑洞洞的入口，门很高，那整个空间的顶也很高，有点像HHN的haunted house，我们在haunted house外面一小段距离的waiting room。不确定这个空间是在室内还是单独一幢楼，甚至不知道它的天花板或者楼顶到底是在哪里，整个建筑/空间都有种很神秘的氛围，我们完全不知道那黑暗里面有什么，所有能看见的只是黑色的，好像光直接被吃掉了。但是我的认知里知道里面有很多谜题，像迷宫之类的，也有各种陷阱谜语，进去要解开那些谜题。我认知里就算我们不知道自己是在迷宫里，其实只要一走进那个黑暗范围内就已经进了迷宫了。但是我同时也知道我和我的团队都是很擅长和喜欢解谜的，没有觉得害怕，反而还特别兴奋准备好了想要进去解谜。感觉好像我们这个团队都像是某种侦探之类的身份（就像最近一直在很努力地开发的游戏《Unfree Freesia》里的主线任务），所以都感觉很刺激很跃跃欲试，认知里理智地知道自己应该感觉害怕或者担心，但是实际上更多是感觉期待和刺激。然后我们就进去了。

下一个记得的场景，转换到一个很日常的画面。我是从第三人称视角看的，好像是从一幢房子里往外看的视角，看到的是门口或者院子，中间有张小圆桌，两边有两把很舒服的椅子，外面天色和风景也都很放松。Dean坐在左边那把椅子上，另一个人坐在右边，感觉就是个很普通很正常的civilian，我知道他不知道hunting和monster和所有那些hunter's life的东西，就是个普通人，但是跟Dean认识了，现在比较熟了。感觉像是熟悉的邻居或者一起工作的人（比如Dean可能在车库或者工地偶尔做些事情认识了的人）。

Dean看起来完全就像现在的Jensen（因为昨天看了很多Jensen在上周末的Creation Convention的panel录像，就是这个样子，很好看；睡前也还在读destiel的同人（虽然没有直接涉及retired life），但是很喜欢那个故事，很长，很投入地读了很多天了，只差最后一点了），非常“aging like fine wine”的好看，大概就是现在的Jensen的年纪，有胡茬，有皱纹，头发比剧里Dean通常要长，但是正好大概就是跟现在的Jensen日常的样子很像，皮肤颜色稍微更tan一些了，也是很fine wine，反正就是完全参考现实的Jensen，但是我又知道他是Dean，不是Jensen，所以按照这个年龄来算大概对Dean来说就是post canon五六年了。

Dean和这个友好的普通人邻居/同事随意地聊天，都是些很日常很生活的话题。然后不知道说到了什么还是发生了什么，反正Dean手里就有了把小刀，可能像缩小版的demon killing knife或者很小的blade，大概不超过一手的长（可能因为我桌上手边经常放着fidget用的可以转的蝴蝶刀式样的碳刀，大概就是那个的长度，但是又知道梦里的是真的有刃的刀）。Dean手里拿着那把小刀，非常娴熟地在手里肌肉记忆地把玩，看起来就是完全没怎么把注意力放在刀上，不担心一不小心会伤到手之类的，手指就直接看起来非常训练有素非常舒服习惯地把刀在手里转来转去地把玩，一边仍然继续在很放松地聊天，整个人身体状态也看起来都仍然是很放松的。

邻居/那另一个男人一边也是继续在跟他聊天，一边注意到了，朝他的手和小刀点头示意说：“I see you know how to handle a weapon.” 说话的语气和潜台词里就是在暗示，他知道Dean肯定有点什么过去，所以才会对武器（从小刀可以很容易地想到手枪，然后就自然推出他肯定对别的武器也都很擅长）这么熟练，可以这么心不在焉就能肌肉记忆地把玩。

Dean很自然很平淡地回答说：“I thought we agreed not to ask about my past.” 非常成熟的aged的状态，非常平淡很peaceful的心态，但是又能很简单很直白地表达了他知道对方在旁敲侧击地想要打听他过去的生活，但是他现在已经retired了，已经从过去的hunting life完全move on了，开始过普通人生活，跟普通人互动聊天，所以他不想再被提醒到过去的事，不想说，不想回忆，把自己跟那些过去切断联系了。现在的他这么peaceful，这么平淡，虽然不想提不想说，但并不是因为trauma，只是单纯move on了，是他自己的选择，他在现在的retirement里已经得到他曾经最想得到的peace了，他现在很content。（然后就闹钟响了。时间掐得正好但是也有点恼没法继续听他们的互动或者听Dean说别的了。然后闹钟在播的晨间广播DJ今天还正好不是平时那位，没有我喜欢的英音了，他请了连续第二天的假，有点不太开心。）

（跟朋友复述讲这段时候本来感觉这个场景里是没有Cas的，但是突然意识我的这个第三人称视角，其实完全可以想成这就是Cas的视角，我和Dean是在我们的房子里，他坐在院子里跟邻居聊天，我站在房子里往外看，留意听他们的对话，看他的侧脸（从我站的那个角度只能看到Dean的大部分右侧脸和邻居的左侧脸，但是我注意力完全都在看Dean），分析他简单一句话里藏的信息和意思，在想他有多好看，在因为他的peace和content而很高兴很满足。所以如果我是Cas，我们是一起在这个房子里过我们的retired life，这还是很符合一般retired fic/domestiel的fic设定的。感觉可以改编。）

(This part only has some vague impression of the feeling it left, not very clear memory of the real visual.) It's a empty dark room, low ceiling, almost mainly black and white, a little bleak, maybe has some tables and chairs in the center. Some windows on the far side of the wall, from ceiling to floor, but still not much light in the whole space. I'm here with a few (one or two, maybe) others that I know I can trust or is part of my team. This space/room is connected to a small corridor then to a way bigger and darker space that we have no idea how big and deep that's going to be. It's like we're in the waiting room outside the entrance of a HHN haunted house. We can see it's very black inside the entrance, tall building, high ceiling or can't even really tell where the ceiling is. I know there's some maze or trap or riddles in there that we need to figure out, I know as soon as we walk in, we'd be inside a maze. But I'm also excited by the idea that the whole thing in there is a big riddle to solve and I really want to solve it, it's like all of us are some sort of detective (like the game I'm working so hard on recently "Unfree Freesia") and I know we are all very good at it, so it's more thrill than any real worry or scare. And so we all rush inside.

Cut to, I'm the third person POV watching Dean sits by some table talking with some other normal random guy. Dean looks like the Jensen of the current age (because I watched Jensen's convention panel recording yesterday, and was reading destiel fic before sleeping, and love that story and has read that for many days now), the very fine wine good looking, aged with time but still very beautiful, rough stubble, hair longer than Dean usually is in the show but similar to what Jensen usually has in the conventions, similar tan on the skin, similar crinkles, just almost perfectly what Jensen look like nowadays, but I just know this is Dean not Jensen. That other guy is probably just a friendly neighbor or coworker, I know he's a normal civilian that doesn't know anything about hunting, and they're just casually chatting.

At one point, maybe something happened, Dean has a blade (or something similar) in his hand, and he grabs and holds it very smoothly, proficiently, unconsciously, purely out of muscle memory (or just instinct), and his hands just look so comfortably and well-trained around the blade (maybe he's just fidgeting with it or something, flipping it around in his hands while chatting, or something). And the neighbor/the other guy clocks it, nods at his hand and blade, saying, "I see you know how to handle a weapon." With the indication in the undertone that he knows Dean must have some sort of past that requires him so well-trained on holding weapons.

Dean answers, very naturally, not uncomfortable at all, not awkward, but also insistent enough to convey the deference: "I thought we agreed not to ask about my past." And just from this simple sentence, I get the context. This is the post-canon retired Dean, living a normal life among normal people now, he's aged and matured, putting all the nasty things behind him, moved on, meet new normal civilian people, and calm and peaceful enough to brush off any mention of the past with such ease, but also knows clearly enough that he doesn't want to be reminded or talk about any of his past, he's very determined to cut the past off him now. And he'd like to keep it that way, just keep this peace and calm and continue with his retirement. He's content in the quietness of the post-canon.
