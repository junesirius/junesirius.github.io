Was rewatching S01-04 before S05 started releasing, and started watching the first 4 episodes of the season in a row, but somehow just suddenly got too busy with something else and couldn't continue, and then all of sudden binged yesterday and finished the whole season. And was damn glad that I didn't watch the last 4 episodes initially, because I wouldn't have been able to pause in the middle, and it was damn depressing.

I started watching Season 1 of the series because it's relaxing and entertaining, and far away from reality so that I can clear my mind and immerging myself in the British farming landscape. Admittedly because of how Jeremy does things, he would make himself stressed out and mess up something every single episode, but that stress is his, not mine, and I can still enjoy my relaxing moments watching him messing around and watching the crops and farm animals. But by now, in Season 5, it's just getting way too realistic and depressing and worrisome that I can't find that familiar relaxing sensation anyway.

Way too much medical situation in this season, starting with Jeremy on hospital bed for a heart surgery, in the middle there's his prostate cancer (which reminds me so much of Cas from "You can keep holding on" and that's an awfully painful memory to remember), and by the end that's another heart surgery and examination. Admittedly the previous few seasons also had some stressful medical situation with other people we've grown to know and get fond of, Allen has heart surgery a few seasons ago, and Gerald had cancer too. But neither is so close and so depressing, maybe because they didn't get as much screen time, so the feeling isn't as intense. I don't like the automatic compassion my brain jumps right into now. And the way Jeremy broke the news about the cancer, that conversation when they were initially just talking about harvesting schedule and weather and crops, and suddenly there's Jeremy saying he's on a tight schedule, he wouldn't be here until this day and that, and when Kaleb was just thinking him as a big man and rich famous man that he is would just be off to London or somewhere doing one of his usual big man's things (like what we, the audience would also naturally think), and suddenly the bomb of cancer got dropped. He dropped it so casually as if it's something he really tried not to disclose but now he has to. And Charlie and Kaleb just got flabbergasted, like the audience do. The background music of the episode just falls quiet. No one see it coming, and no one likes how it's going. And it's almost all so clear and easy to guess the location of the cancer from the way he answers to Kaleb's "Where" is a dismissive "It's none of anyone's business" because he knows he's broadcasting and people all over the world is gonna see and know it, so he's not going to say it. But he ends up still let it out when he's done with the therapy. Of course it's prostate, where else could it be for it to be more coincidental (as the way I know and I had read) and dramatic (from the way how often he'd tease about balls and animal sexual activities).

He looks so tired and old in the first shot when he comes back from hospital. All those grey white beard and messy hair, not able to cleaning himself up during the time, and from the painful grimace when the tractor bounces on the farm road and how much it must has hurt the fresh wound of his surgery for the cancer (exactly the way I read and learned from "You can keep holding on", I just basically replayed the who 500k something fic in my head with all the cinematic effects). The compassion almost overwhelms me, and I don't like it. I'm not watching this show for feeling painful. I want the old time leisure fun back. If next season isn't going to be any lighter, I'd guess I'd still watch it, because knowing me, I wouldn't be able to let it go, but I'd suspect I'd like it even less.
