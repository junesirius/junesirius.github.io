## S01

### S01E01

The very first episode, the very first impression: It's a show of a wealthy man, and he has no intention of hiding how wealthy he is. He just completely doesn't care about how much money he'd spend on farming at all. The whole point of the whole show and all the things he's done in those episodes, is just to have some fun and make enough bullshit things happening around the farm to have enough for the documentary content full. (which, isn't a technically critique per say, seeing wealthy people doing all those weird things with their money and getting into trouble is also one of the many main points of the fun, but the jealousy of the wealth can sometimes still create some unfairness feeling and making the watching not as enjoyable and entertaining as they're supposed to be.)

So the very first thing he spent money on, as soon as he starts on farming, he's squandering, almost in a way like showing off, by buying a Lamborghini tractor. But truthfully speaking, it does look quite nice and big and powerful. I can see why wealthy people would like that.

So there's a second angle of enjoying the show: for once, almost like a reality show, the viewer (me) gets to pretend I'm that wealthy too and think in their mindset what would I do with those amount of money. Buying the land and farming and coming up with all those weird ideas certainly aren't the *worst* way of spending those money. And admittedly, he does put honest exhausting work into that life, so that's quite physical and respectable too. Which makes the whole show more entertaining again.

Let alone all the nature view really is astonishing. Totally understand why people would want to retire to British countryside like that.

### S01E05

The farm shop is a complete testimony of his wealth and celebrity status, it's just unfair.

Initially thought that farm shop really isn't much, the only abundant thing they can sell is potato! But just because he has however-many-million followers on Twitter and he posted about it, there are so many cars and traffic and people coming to buy those things and just to have a chance to talk to him. It's not the credit of the farm or the farm shop, he's totally monetizing his own fame and celebrity status and he completely is aware of it, and very much enjoys exploiting it! The same can be said about the restaurant later in S02. I can totally see why the villagers would be worried and the local shire counsel would be pissed about him. It's disaster to the original old little farm and its old little classic way of living, but I agree no one knows if that level of economical boost is a bless or disaster, so maybe it helps, maybe it's not. People does have all the reasons to be concerned, but at the end of the day, it doesn't look too bad, so, kudos to that.

### S01E08

Love his complete lack of knowledge of anything farming related, so Charlie and Caleb have to explain everything from scratch and teach him hand in hand, which makes the show perfectly amazing and educational for all the other people who also have absolutely no knowledge in agriculture.

Pretty sure I got some weird knowledge saved in my brain somewhere after watching the show, even though I might not realize it. Something something about the moisture level before you harvest, or you have to have trim line every eight turns, or things about where to store the hay and seeds and potato can stay fresh under soil for a little bit without their leaves, or lambs and sheep and cooked mutton with beans and potato makes a perfect decent British shepherd's dinner (which makes me want to eat too).

But also admittedly, Jeremy does have a vast knowledge about anything else, journalism wise. All those little random comments and analogies he made, up to politics and big topics, down to cars and other small topics, he does look very knowledgeable and capable, which totally explained his level of success and wealthiness. So guess success people and celebrity does have their capital to have what they have. Listening to him talking does help you learn a lot, and many of the Voice Overs (though these parts might be postprocessing and very likely scripted) are so wise you can feel the dripping wisdom from it.

And the season 1 went by so much faster than expected. It's almost as if spending just the same amount of labor with them instead of just watching him doing all the hard work. The bees and wasabi and sheep and building a wild lake, so many random others that aren't strictly simple straightforward farming might be the exact things that made the show so unbelievably attractive and different from others, because admit it, who in their right mind would be like that? So guess being ignorant has its own benefit too, at least they had so much fun in the farm (and we had so much fun in front of the TV). Did I say "attractive"? I totally mean this show is addictive.

## S02

### S02E04

He basically hit 2 cars in 40 minutes time. In the same episode. And all he really does is curse into the camera, and then the life moves on. Can't imagine how much that would cost, but from the way he doesn't seem really care that much, as if that's not even a scratch in his vast fortune, the utter uncaring of any expense and financial loss is becoming envious. We've long known the man is rich, but not really *how* rich. And this is basically him answering, *very*.

Hate the scene about him almost cutting his finger off peeling potato, because... well, that's basically me, and I know it's destined to happen before it really happened. Because... every time I use that thing it would happen, and if he's also one of those clumsy people like me who doesn't know how to work my way around the kitchen, then, yeah, it's just destined to happen. And the camera just *had to* show a closeup of the bloody finger and the surgery immediately afterwards is just uncomfortable. Remind me to skip this part next time I rewatch this episode again.
