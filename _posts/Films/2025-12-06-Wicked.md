Rewatched the first Wicked movie right before watching this, and oh god, the ending, the confession, those feelings and crying, the confession before the farewells. I can't. Why do I have to see destiel in EVERY SINGLE MOVIE and shows I watch lately? Is the universe just crowding me with destiel because I'm obsessed, or because I'm obsessed so I'm seeing them in everything? It can't be coincidental that this is yet another story of falling in love with your best friend, then there's the "I love you" before saying goodbye and crying. Love the scene they cry on the two sides of the same door at the same time, their hands hovering on the door in the same place, then touch their lips. It's a kiss that never happened. It broke my heart so completely.

And then the "not really dead" ending. At least all of them survived. I should feel glad, but why do I feel more sad? They'll never see each other again. Galinda never knows her best friend is still alive. And Elphie would never be able to see her again. Is it any better than death, really? Or is it more torturing to know the forever grief and aching would torture both of them until they're dead?

Now I really have to read the books now, see what the book really said about what happened after.

Though, after watching the musical, has to admit, as much as I love the movie, I love the musical so much more. The stage transition has a different texture from the movie scene transition, and some transition can be quicker and more abrupt and still makes sense, while the movie really has to fill in the logic gaps, the stage and change of setting would have already taken care of that. The music is better in the musical too, of course. Enjoy the music so much that I can't stop looping it over and over and over again.

Though there is one song that's more powerful in the movie that makes my hair stand and blood rushing to my ears -- two, technically, but feels like the tune is similar enough they might just be the variation of each other (I can be wrong): the near-ending one in the first Wicked movie when Elphie made the decision of become the "wicked witch" that everyone says she is, she flies into the sky with all the flying monkeys floating in the air behind her, that powerful tone hits right into my brain; and the one in the second movie when Elphie cries for Fiyero, and the scene intercuts between her painful cries and curse casting and Fiyero's burning, the intensity and pain burns my heart, my heart bleeds for all of them and my tears drop the first time I get to this part. All of them are powerless against how the events turn out, however hard they try, it seems something always went wrong, someone always get hurt. It's not fair, and they cry about the pain in their song. It's too powerful to bear. Too beautiful not to listen. So I relisten and rewatch and cry for so many more times. It hurts so good and beautifully.
