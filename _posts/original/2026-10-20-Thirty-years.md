I will lose my mind, one day

I just know it

I may not die by then, but I'd already lose my memory, my mind

I'd already stop being me

I just know it

<br>

My beautiful, beautiful brain

My mind, my head, my thoughts, my memory

Everything that's making me me

I'd lose it before I'm perished physically

Becoming a husk, an empty vessel

<br>

Because that's exactly what ran in the family

And that's going to happen to me

<br>

My beautiful beautiful brain is always racing and running and processing and spinning

The dancing ballerina on the music box

Spinning so fast it turns into a shadow

Spinning and losing itself

As if it knows before I do, that it's running out of time

<br>

It's already happening, the loss of memory

Fast and startle

<br>

I reach to my memory and found large blocks of blank, everywhere I look

I blocked them out for the pains I don't want to relive

Until there is nothing left

I didn't even make the decision to do that

My brain made the decision for me

<br>

The same way it flinched away from the language it grew up with

Because the pain and weight finally become too much to bear

<br>

If it's not for memory, for what are we living?

If nothing gets to leave behind, for what are we doing all of them?

I grabbed each thread of my thoughts so desperately

Writing them down in any form, on any surface

Desperate to make a mark, leave a trace

Because I know, I *know*

I'd lose them

Sooner or later

I know

<br>

They pass through my head like birds across sky

Writing them down

Writing everything down in a panick when I can still remember

Because if I don't

They'd be gone before I remember they were ever there

<br>

And I only have such short time left

Thirty years and no more, if not even shorter

Because it's fated

It's carved in my gene

In the cells in my brain

In my destiny

<br>

My brilliant and amazing brain

Spinning and burning bright

Only because that's all the time it knows it would ever get

It's burning up so quickly

And there's nothing to stop it

<br>

So all it can do

Is to try very very hard to do everything while it still can

While it still has time

While it can still remember

While I'm still me

<br>

Thirty years

That's all I'd get

Every second beyond that would be stolen time

If not shorter

There were already thirty wasted years

And another ten budgeted out for nothing

Just to bargain for a life in the remaining twenty

To *really live the life*, in that remaining twenty

That would wither away so quickly

<br>

I'm running out of time

Tic, toc, time is up

Tic, toc, the monster is closer, eighty-two minutes away [1]

Tic, toc, the demon's deal is up and they'll be here to collect [2]

<br>

How do you spend each single second

When you get the spoiler of the total amount?

Is that a curse or a blessing to know at all?

The crossroad deal that I already know the price

But never know what I get in return

Only that when the time is up, in thirty years

Time will be up and the demon will return

<br>

Just found out about this thirty-year deal on my brain

A count-down bomb buried inside every single cell

Underneath every thread of thought and memory

Wiping them away one day at a time

A finger on the big red button that says "Erase"

And I can do nothing about it

And there is no getting out of the deal

No way of defusing the bomb

Or stopping the countdown

Thirty years, and that's all there is

<br>

In the most silent night of the deepest hour

The clock is still ticking

It's always ticking

Tic, toc

<br>

[1] Reference of *Doctor Who*, S09E11, where the Doctor runs away from the monster and each loop is only eighty-two minutes long, before time is up and the monster catches up. Before that, you can only keep running, and running, and running.

[2] Reference for *Supernatural*, S03, where Dean has one year of life left from the crossroad deal, before the hellhound comes to collect his soul.
