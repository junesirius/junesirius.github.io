July was difficult. But now we passed it.

May was even more difficult. But we passed it, and it was already three months ago.

We live in the ruins of battlefields, burned down and deserted, full of muddy tracks from war chariots.

Some of the battles we won, some of them we lost. But all of them left ruins behind. Bodies of the past, haunting the ground like a ghost of yesterday, like a bad dream.

We shift from one battlefield to the next. Constant. There are always new ones to meet, some to kill, some to kill with. Some got forgotten, some got remembered forever. Some live, some stop.

We move, burning down one land then the next, as the sun rises and sets. We move with shadows, letting them tire us out, swallow us the same way they swallow the bodies falling down. But by dawn, we move on again.

The battlefield moves backward when we are too tired to move forward. The bodies lie down when we are too tired to fight. This happens with or without our will. We move on, or we get left behind, forever. Lying among the bodies, becoming someone else's long-forgotten dream.

Every day is a battlefield. So is every yesterday, but they will pass; they always do. All of them lie under our feet. As long as we are moving, as long as we are standing, we are moving forward, toward where the sun rises. We have nothing but tomorrow.
