I'm itching out of my skin again. It's getting familiar, that feeling as if something is growing under my skin, crawling and stretching, like those old *Alien* movies, it's like something alive impregnated inside me, swelling and growing, like a life coming to be from a seed. I can't possibly imagine what pregnancy is like, but right now, somehow, I can relate. That thing is coming into shape, coming to be something that can move around inside me, waiting for a chance to bite a tear on my skin to crawl out, and I can do nothing to stop it or control what time it decides to come out. It crawls out of my skin of its own will, only because I wanted to put it off for longer than it's pleased with.

It's itching to death when that thing is crawling and swimming under the skin, growling at me for not letting it out, I would scare for my life for the day it decides to be impatient and not care about my real boring adult life that has other "important" things scheduled and planned, it just comes out, disrupting all those plans, demanding all my attention and will, and I can do nothing but allow it, sit down and write down the story it has turned into (from the seed of the vague idea that it once was). Only then, after it's written onto papers, turning into words that I can see, that it can see, only then does it satisfy for a little while and the itch that drove me crazy can ease for a few hours, a few days if I'm lucky (but when was I ever lucky?), until the next round of idea takes seed and the little bastard monster that is the life of story starts growing again.

I figure it's a gift of some sort, some people never get that feeling that creation is survival, that stories grow like a real baby, a lifeform inside you, you can almost feel it physically; I figure I should be appreciated for it (I do, most of the time, some of the time, not always though, not all the time).

Every time it happens, I feel like my body, my physical form, is nothing more than a vessel, a container for the ideas and stories and whatever is happening in my brain. My spirit and soul need a place to live, to navigate, to write those down as real words and letters, more physical than the ideas that reside in my head. I don't really live in this body, but I need the body to contain those that won't survive without one.

Oddly, I relate to Cas so much now, I feel less human this way. I can totally relate to him not being a human but having to reside inside one to move around the Earth. It's annoying at times; it's frustrating that sometimes the needs of this vessel overwhelm you stupidly and take up more time than you like to give it. If I could, I'd rather create and consume and let my head take all the time it needs without having to feed energy to this vessel, letting it rest, going through the complicated process of substance and water circulation. "Don't you get tired of urination?" Cas asked. And I feel him. I do. (Eating and sleeping too, and keeping it clean and healthy. The amount of time and effort just to keep this vessel functioning is incredible.)
