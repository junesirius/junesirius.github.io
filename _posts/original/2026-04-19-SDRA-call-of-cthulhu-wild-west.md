### PS

potluck recipe for 6-8 (ingredients and time)

ingredients:

- romaine lettuce x1
- cucumber x1
- corn cob x1 (optional)
- cavatappi pasta x2 cups (any pasta)
- firm tofu x1 box
- ground beef x1 box (80+20)
- optional seasoning (lemon, pepper, soy sauce, pasta sauce, feta cheese)

steps:

1. prepare two pots, one for water, one for oil
2. one pot boil water
3. one full romaine lettuce, washed, cut to small pieces, throw into water pot, dip 30s, take out, drain water, cool down with cold water, put in pan
4. one full cucumber, washed, peeled, cut to small pieces, throw into water pot, dip 30s, take out, drain water, cool down with cold water, put in pan, mixed
5. (optional) one corn cob, washed, peeled, get corn kernels from cob, same as above (water pot, 30s, take out, drain, cool down, mix)
6. a new pot of water, boiled
7. 2 cups of cavatappi pasta into the pot, timer set 12+2 min, without lid; once done and soft, drain off water, wash with cold water, put into pan and mix with others
8. meanwhile, oil pot add olive oil, heat up
9. a box of firm tofu into small pieces, when oil is hot, put into oil pot/pan, wait until sizzling/boiling, add pepper + soy sauce, drain water/oil, put into pan
10. quickly wash/wipe the oil pot, new olive oil, wait until heated
11. a box of ground beef (80+20), optional cut loose, put into oil, stir and split them into small pieces, wait until no red, turn off fire, add pepper + soy sauce, drain off oil, wash with some water, put into pan, mix
12. water pot, boil new water
13. a box of cherry tomato (\~20), cut in half, put into water pot, wait 30-60s, drain water, cool down with cold water, put into pan and mix

Mix everything some more, don't close lid until cool down.

Optional seasoning while eating: feta cheese (very salty), pasta tomato sauce, Mozzarella cheese, lemon drops
