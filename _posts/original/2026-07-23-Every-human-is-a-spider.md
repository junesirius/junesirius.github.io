Every human is like a spider. we tie our net on something, on someone else; we hang onto them so that when wind comes, the cobweb shakes, but it doesn't break as long as our holds are still standing. When those people leave us, one by one, the cobweb loses its footing. Every loss loosens it. With every loss, the net shakes more, loses one point of connection to the ground, to the world. Until the last one is gone, and a wind comes, the web flies away.
