Ever since I set my search engine default to only return results from before 12/31/2019 and absolutely no AI summary, I'm finally able to find anything useful and real. And not contaminating my brain or my reading ability.

Is the search result outdated? Maybe, a little bit. But unless I have to search current world news, I don't usually need any of the new things people and AI invented in the past seven years. And to be honest, let's face it, there's way too much more useless information than useful information out there invented in the past seven years, and the rubbish gibberish articles and useless stuff cluttering the internet are exploding exponentially in the past year alone. Might as well filter everything out to let the real answer not drown in the trash.

It is so amazingly simple and clean and not exhausting to search now.

This is how, if you want that blessing of peace of no-AI in your search engine too: [Remove Annoying Google AI From Search Results | YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=555Lc2dwp3E).

It's not my video, just a reference I found, and I modified it to fit my needs. If you copy the string under that video, you'll get rid of the AI overview, but the returned results would still include all the recent webpages (most of which have articles that are just so clearly and frustratingly AI-generated).

To limit the returned results to only include things from before 12/31/2019, use this string instead (to put into the place where the video would teach you how and where):

```text
{google:baseURL}search?q=%s&tbs=cdr:1,cd_max:12/31/2019
```

The funny thing is, after setting that, and then if you ask google how to get rid of AI summary, there is nothing there, google would be like: "What AI summary are you talking about? There is no AI summary." Because there wasn't one before 2020, which is exactly what I want.
