When I have a house (which I don't think will ever happen, but if for some reason it does), I want an old-time hand typewriter, a DVD player, a vintage soundbox player, and decorate the house in 80s style, and the TV is a clumsy box TV, and garage is huge with at least one Impala, one DeLorean, and one Harley motorcycle and it's red. It will be near the beach and sea, and miles without anyone else, private, no tourist, no neighbor. And old-style clumsy radio player (that makes creepy white noise when it doesn't receive signals). But no old-style grandfather clock (absolutely not). Chandelier isn't necessary and isn't preferred. No mirror unless absolutely needed. Butler is okay, if he's like Alfred, not a serial killer. Serial killer is okay if he's like Corinthian. No maid. Enormous gigantic library with shelves to the ceiling and all the books. Large storage rooms with marks and runes and mysterious symbols on boxes of weird items. Dungeon with chains and locks, hidden behind secret door. Astronomy room with huge telescope pointing at sky. Art room with painting supplies. Music room with instruments. Outdoor garden with grass you can lie down on. Two rocking chairs. No wind-bell. Has paintings on the wall but none with human profiles unless fictional.

Did I just give a basic description of a mixture of Bunker and Stranger Things' houses because 80s style looks cool in those? Or was I trying to rebuild my mind palace *again* just because I was watching old movies? (But they *are* looking really cool and aesthetic and better than whatever the modern style is, if the modern version even has a *style*.)

Call it rich man's fantasy. Might need more than Aladdin's genie or wishing willow to make it come true. But who knows? One can dream. Actually, no, not really even want to dream about it. Not even gonna make it an unrealistic motivation or anything like that, might not even be that happy to own what I call dream house, I don't even know why I put that down in the middle of the night right before falling into dreams. Good imagination practice, at most, perhaps. To use as a set for some story of some kind. My imaginary self's imaginary house. Or maybe just because someone talked about house earlier today and that triggered the subconsciousness start thinking about the idea. Not that it would ever come true or that's really what the heart really wanted anyway.
