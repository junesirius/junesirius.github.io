session topic: Making Film Magic: From Script to Screen in 2026

session description: You'd like to break through to have a successful film career, but where do you begin, and what should you know up front? It's time for a frank, honest discussion about the dos and don'ts from those industry pros who have done it before. Award-winning writer/filmmaker Tim Chizmar (First Comics News, director of Naked Alien Massacre, WWE ghostwriter) moderates this panel of industry professionals who will tell you the truth about adapting your ideas from a piece of literature or comic series into a successful visual medium.
