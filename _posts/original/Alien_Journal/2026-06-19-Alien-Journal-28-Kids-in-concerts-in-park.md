Concerts in park

there are so many kids there, running around, mostly blonde, mostly girls, but also a few boys, a few non-blonde, some very athletic, some artistic, some can dance, some just running around chasing each other to have fun

a story where those kids dancing/playing in the park during the concerts, they only just meet/find/know each other randomly because of the concerts, but then they getting familiar and become friends and that friendship last for life, all only because of the very casual summertime "concerts in park" community activity. there might be romantic stories as well, they sort of just grow up together, with similar (or different) music taste, then realize the love is more than friendship, then some angst in realization and fear and confession, and get together just as comfortable as they are friends, including same gender, including sexuality realization as they grow older

but mostly still always have a theme centered around music and park and picnic because that's how everything (all the little serendipity and miracles) started
