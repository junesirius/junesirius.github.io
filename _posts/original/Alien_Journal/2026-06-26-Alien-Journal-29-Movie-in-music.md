(story where music is alive, all consuming, possessing, using human as media)

brass band, concert in park

This is the first time I know music can force image into my brain, making me see movies I don't know I'd ever watch. The images and sound work perfectly together, everything automatic, so automatic that I feel like a media, a machine, just a platform to stream them, my brain is not my own, but a tool for the art to flow through.

Probably because of how brass music was used in films and I've watched too many of them without thinking about them, and now, everything that's been saved in the subconscious comes back to me, showing up in my conscious, ripping me of any ability of thinking for myself. My brain is hijacked so severely that the only thing it can do is to watch the movie the music pours onto me. The overwhelming feeling reminds me of the old bad joke: "If I'm still thinking, it means you aren't doing your job right", well, the brass band and their music is certainly doing their job right.

The first song is slow, melodic, a little sad. I don't have enough words to describe the music by itself, but I can describe the scene it created in my head, it forced into my mind. I see a woman in a dark room, standing by the window. It's all dark in the whole frame, old time vintage looking, 30s, maybe, like black and white footage, or maybe the early time when color is invented but pale. Lighting is limited, but from the window where light goes through, you can still see the woman is beautiful, and sad, and alone. Wearing a white dress. Lonely. Then she turns, looking outside the window, down there on the street, a man is walking quickly, away from the house she's in. You know this is the man that just left her place. She looks at his back until he's around the corner and gone. She moves back inside the room, having a music player on, a slow, slow song. She closes her eyes, starts dancing and spinning in the middle of an empty room. The white dress spins around her. She's like a lonely flower, blossoming, with no one seen it but herself. The only color in this image is her red lips, so red that it looks like blood cutting from a sharp blade and a thin cut.

The second song is faster paced, jumping, active. I see a man running down the narrow street. The street is ancient, like those in Rome, or Egypt, the walls and buildings around them are dusty sandy yellow color. The buildings look as if they're made of sand, but they're standing just fine. The man is a thief, running with panic. He's trying to escape, he's not looking where he's running. He's ducking into any narrow small passageway he can find, trying to get rid of the man chasing after him. The chaser wears uniform, a more clumsy man, bigger in shape. He has to push people aside to move quickly. He can't run as fast, but he still manages to keep up, maybe because he's familiar with the streets. The thief gets himself cornered in a narrow alley without an out. The chaser is getting closer. The thief panics, but then he starts climbing up the building along its outer wall. When the chaser gets to the same cornered alley, the thief is already standing on top of the building, starting to run from the rooftops again. The chaser climbs after him, clumsy again, but still somehow manages to keep up. The chasing continues on the rooftop. The thief has to jump across the buildings, and he leaps, but he misses. The chaser catches up and finally catches him. The two of them are panting heavily while both of them are practically kneeling and tumbling to the ground while the thief tries his last try of struggling and the chaser manages to cuff him.

The third song feeds a peaceful summer image into my head. I see a summer afternoon by the pool. Everything is so peaceful and calm. The sun is bright on the sky, scorchingly hot. The pool is so blue, water shaking and moving as the people in the pool playing. The camera moves alongside the pool, looking at the ladies lying under the big sunbathing umbrellas. They're the mothers and wives and other free young ladies, all of them beautiful like models, cheerful and chatty. They are drinking little colorful drinks with those beautiful little umbrellas. Rich people. The mothers are paying half their attention to the kids near and in the pool. Some kids just jump into the water, having huge splashes. And then we realize it's actually not pool, it's beach. The view zooms out, it's so broad out there, still calm and peaceful, still summer afternoon. Lazy and sleepy.

The last one that used my head as a media is a complicated one. So complicated that the image in my head splits into two panels like a comic shot, or a split screen movie that I've only ever really see in anime but I imagine movies can also do if they want. I see a man in a graveyard, digging through the graves; and then I see the same man in a party, that kind of rich people top class fancy party where everyone dresses fancy expensive formal suits with free wines and champagnes and platter food, and the man is moving among the guests, wearing suit just like them, but we all know he doesn't belong there even though he tries so hard to blend in. And then the scene becomes split screen ever since, fast paced, both scenes intense, left is the grave, right is the party. The man is running in both; he's running in the graveyard because there's the light swinging around, almost lighting him up, maybe that would mean gatekeepers or police or someone might find him and chase after him, and he doesn't want to be caught, because he's a grave thief, digging up the graves looking for anything that he can sell; he's running so recklessly without really looking at where he's going; around him it's getting darker again, all the gravestones standing on the grass, it's a dark night. On the right side he's moving along the guest, he's playing cards and tricks for them, amusing some of them, talking with some of the gentlemen, pretending to be witty and knowledgeable, and he's very charming and good-looking, from his demeanor you'd never have imagined that's the same man in the graveyard just at the nighttime digging bodies and stealing, but this *is* the same man. Right now in the party he's putting on the rich man's mask and pretending to be one of them, and some (most) of them actually buy it, believed him, because he's very charming and easy to talk to. But what he's actually doing is he's a con man/magician in the party. He's getting their trust, but he'll soon do the tricks that helps him gain money from these rich old men who wouldn't even know how their money get stolen. He'd get money so easily (but also still risky) this way than doing the heavy labors in the graveyard. So maybe the graveyard shot isn't exactly parallel in the time-sense, maybe it can be a past parallel, like that's what he used to do but not the exact thing he did the night before the party. But there's still so much parallel and comparison and difference can be seen from the split screen, and the moving pace in both scenes align perfectly with the music. The contrast in color (the left is all dark and fuzzy, the right is colorful and way too bright to almost dizzy) is also something interesting to see. Sometimes he'd have to move quickly among the guests too, he'd tell the gentleman he's talking to and almost succeed in conning that he had to go (which he really doesn't want to give up yet), but he has to if he wants to stay in the party without getting caught, because he notices someone is already moving from outside the guest crowd toward him (while trying not to be noticed, but he noticed them anyway), maybe that someone is the same one from the graveyard chasing scene, the police or gatekeeper from back then, that's how they recognize him and not getting fooled by his current disguise. And he has to pretend he's still just one of the guests, having a glass of alcohol in his head, but moving quickly, keep saying "Excuse me" to pass someone else, pretending he's just too eager to go to somewhere (like maybe just an urgent need to go to bathroom), while keep looking back to check if the chaser is getting any close.
