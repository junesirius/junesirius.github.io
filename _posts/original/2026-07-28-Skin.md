Once again I want to hurt my skin, but for different reason from five years ago. Five years ago, I want to feel pain, I want it as a punishment. I want to feel alive because I was dying everyday.

But now, it's a different reason.

Now I want to tear it open like bird breaking the shell from inside. I want to crawl out of this skin, so literal that I want to break it apart, cut an opening, like an exit in the darkness, like a crack where the light comes in, like snake's skin. Too tight, too small, everywhere. To peel it off, to free the things living underneath that's suffocating. I want to break free, and the skin is the cage. The pain will be freedom.
