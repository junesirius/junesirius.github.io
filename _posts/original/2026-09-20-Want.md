I want something.

It's not too pricy, but it's not too cheap, either.

It's not exactly necessary; just that I kinda want it.

And that's the problem.

I can't seem to find a good excuse to get an approval.

"I'll get it as my early birthday gift," I suggest.

"Thought you said you'd stop celebrating your birthday from this year -- we're still gonna talk about this some time, that you trying to use it as some kind of self-punishment. But if we stick to that, you're not gonna get any birthday gift," you answer.

"Early Christmas gift, then," I say.

I get all my friends Christmas gifts. Some prepared months ahead.

"Do you believe in Christian belief? Or Santa Claus?" You tilt your head.

"Thanksgiving, then!" My patience starts running out.

"I don't know there's custom to exchange gifts in Thanksgiving, outside of stuffed turkey," you answer plainly.

"I'll give that to myself as a reward, then." My patience is stretched very thin now.

"Did you do anything recently that earned you such a reward?" You look at me straight in the eyes.

"For Christ's sake, I just want the goddamn thing!" I stomp and snap, finally exploding. "Can't I just have the goddamn thing without a festival or some bullshit reason?!"

"Atta boy." You smile, out of my surprise.

"That's what I've been waiting to hear all this time," you say.

You just wanna see how long it's gonna take for me to just want something, for me, without an excuse. I realize.

It's not easy.

It doesn't come naturally.

It took me long.

And it will still take me very, very long to fix that.

But for now,

I'm gonna get that goddamn thing I want.

Just because I want it.
