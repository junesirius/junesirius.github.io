On the first day, God thinks she needs to create light. But she feels a minor discomfort in her stomach, so she decides to take it easy and do it tomorrow.

On the second day, the discomfort turns into severe pain. God lies on the bed, can't even move an inch. She can feel the bleeding, she's sweating, she's nauseous and hungry at the same time. She doesn't have time to think about work; she spends most of the day sleeping and resting. She decides she'll do the work the next day.

On the third day, the pain eases a little, going back to a discomfort. She leaves the bed, feeding herself with some food. She decides she's recovered enough to catch up on work for a little bit, so she creates light. That exhausts her again, so she turns in early to get rest.

On the fourth day, the discomfort is very minor, but the bleeding continues. She does all the work from sitting on her favorite softest armchair, and separates the sky. She's falling behind on the work, but she's still not 100%. She decides to catch up on work the next day.

On the fifth day, the bleeding is minor enough to be forgotten. She separates land from water; she sets the sun, the moon, and the stars; she creates birds and fish. She works all the way until midnight and feels burned out again. She realizes she should have rested early too this day, but at least she's pleased she caught up with her working schedule. She goes to bed resting at two in the morning.

On the sixth day, the worst has passed. She gets up around noon and is glad to see the bleeding has ended. She goes to stretch and does a few exercises that she missed for a week. Then she goes back to work, creating land creatures and humans that look like her. She pays especially close attention to the little humans that biologically assemble her, and replicate all the biological features. She thinks about removing the routine pain and bleeding for them, but on second thought, thinking back on her own week, she leaves it there, thinking it's a very important aspect of life that they shouldn't miss. If she has to endure it every month, so shall those human women. It's an emotional day for her.

On the seventh day, she wakes up feeling good because there is no work waiting for her and she has no pain. It's a nice day, so she declares this her favorite day of the week.

<br>

God is a girl, and she has menstruation during the week of creation.
