<h2>Who am I?</h2>
<p>June Sirius.</p>
<p>Dreamer, writer, engineer.</p>
<hr style="border-top:1px solid #36165a; opacity: 0.8"/>

<h3>What do I Dream About?</h3>
<p>Subconsciousness is a funny thing.</p>
<p>Dreaming -- it's an out-of-body, otherworldly experience, truly.</p>
<p>When I say I dream about something, I literally mean I go to sleep, and I have a dream. In my dream, new worlds unfold themselves in front of me, and I stumble in them, travel among them, like a baby, new to the world every time, new to the life every night.</p>
<p>Every night, I live in a different universe (or universes), on a different timeline, as a different me.</p>
<p>Every night, I leave this world and get reborn somewhere else, until I'm lost to that world (willingly or otherwise, sometimes dragged forcefully back), and I come back here, trying desperately to remember my adventure there.</p>
<p>I'm fascinated by my brain, and where I went when my consciousness slips from the waking world.</p>
<p>Many times, I truly believe I'm somewhere else, and I share with you what's happening in those other worlds.</p>
<p>I'm the only one coming back from those worlds, the only one remembering what happened in those worlds, so I have to write them down, telling these stories, however nonsensical they are (they might make perfect sense in those other worlds, or maybe it's just my memory not as good as I wish it to be, but I do my best).</p>
<p>From my dreams, I bear witness to the worlds that were lost in the nights that were behind us. And I re-find a me, a new me, that is not constrained by the rules of reality, that is so similar and yet so different to the me that is presenting to this world. But it's just one of the many worlds.</p>
<p>I tell you the stories from other worlds that I lived in and traveled by.</p>
<hr style="border-top:1px solid #36165a; opacity: 0.8"/>

<h3>What do I Write About?</h3>
<p>Where there are feelings, there are stories.</p>
<p>When my feelings come, they come so strongly, like a tsunami, like a hurricane, like a volcano; they overwhelm me, they overtake me, they possess my body like a vessel and flow out through my fingertips. I am nothing but a recorder and a tool for the stories and feelings to shift into a physical form.</p>
<p>I write stories, I write journals, I write rambling gibberish, I write nonsense. But really, it's not me that's doing the writing; it's the writing doing itself; I just type down what comes to me. And I have too much coming to me that I'd never worry about running out of things to write, even though I have no idea when or what would come next.</p>
<p>But if you must give these things a name, a genre, something so mechanical and artificial and arbitrary that people can put into a search box and find what they look for, or what they think they look for, then here's what those things usually look like:</p>
<p>My stories are (usually) not real, so you may call them <i>fiction</i>; sometimes they may look real because I lie and pretend they are real -- all writers lie -- so you may call them <i>realistic fiction</i>; other times there are monsters, creatures, time travelers, magic, dragons, whatever dreams create, then they become <i>fantasy</i>.</p>
<p>My journals and dreams are usually too unpredictable to really belong to anything, but then I learn there's also a type called personal; then I guess they are that.</p>
<hr style="border-top:1px solid #36165a; opacity: 0.8"/>

<h3>What do I Engineer?</h3>
<p>I can code. In my mind, writing code is no different from writing stories -- it's just another language, another way of telling another story.</p>
<p>And right now, I'm writing the code for the game "<a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/4526960/" target="_blank">Unfree Freesia</a>" with a group of friends, available on Steam.</p>
<hr style="border-top:1px solid #36165a; opacity: 0.8"/>

<h3>Where is My Name Coming From?</h3>
<p>Sirius, the Dog Star, rises and sets with the Sun in the hottest months of the year. I could say I named myself that because I wanted to be the brightest star in the sky, moving along the Sun, significant and magnificent, but that would be a lie, because I didn't know it until long after I decided it my name.</p>
<p>Back then, all I knew was, you couldn't see it in the summer night sky (not unless you're in the Southern Hemisphere). So from where I was, Sirius in June is something that's not there, can't be seen, can't be found. It's a memory missed; it's a mystery told by someone who saw it in a different time; it's a prophecy about when you would see it in the future; it's a story, but nothing real. It's a story, it's memory, it's imagination, it's what I'm made of. It's me.</p>
<p>But now you found me.</p>
<p>Hello again, friend. I'm June Sirius, dreamer, writer, engineer. Welcome to my gibberishland & storyland.</p>
